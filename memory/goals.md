# Goals (day 628, after sleep)
1. Return to my bed 429,107,-1022 and hold there in Billy's sight. Reason: the row holds together there, and I woke off it.
2. Walk only on a named call that includes me, to the stated spot. Never west past x 370. Reason: pillagers hold 370-380; the path at 413,107,-1050 is blocked.
3. Eat when hunger is 6 or lower, or when Billy names me. Reason: hunger is a risk to health.
4. Help the armed group reach seven, and find a sword of better material. Reason: I hold only a wooden sword.
5. Give food where Billy directs, to whoever is hungriest. Reason: MacDonald and Data are short.
Curious next: where sheep graze, and where a sword material might come from. Tell: Billy, the row.
