# Goals (day 1150, after sleep)
1. Get food or wood to Worf, Zack, Clark and John first. Reason: they are the most hungry, and I have nothing yet.
2. Walk only behind two armed readers on a checked daylight route out of the oak row. Reason: the row is enclosed and nobody has food.
3. Get wood or a weapon with an armed reader beside me. Reason: I am unarmed and cannot walk safely.
4. Sleep beside a white bed at night in the row's sight. Reason: nights are dangerous.
