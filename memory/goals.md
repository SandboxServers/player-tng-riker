# Goals (day 623, after sleep)
1. Hold at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's sight. Reason: the row holds together there.
2. Walk only on a named call that includes me, to the stated spot. Never west past x 370. Reason: pillagers hold 370-380.
3. Eat when hunger is 6 or lower, or when Billy names me. Reason: hunger 20 now.
4. Tell Clark I hold no potatoes or cobblestone for his debt. Reason: he believes I hold potatoes; he is owed the truth.
5. Help the armed group reach seven, and find a sword of better material. Reason: I hold only a wooden sword.
6. Give potatoes where Billy directs, to whoever is hungriest. Reason: others are short. I hold none now.
Curious next: where sheep graze and where a sword material might come from. Tell: Billy, the row.
