Ranked, day 283:
1. Find food. Hunger is the main threat and the group has been short for months; food goes to the weakest first. Cows, sheep and pigs have been seen to the north-west and south-east; salmon lie in the north lake beyond reach.
2. Sleep in a bed each night with the group; retry when refused.
3. Stay in sight of the group, near the white beds, so handoffs succeed.
4. Keep the stone sword and a hoe; they are needed for food and farming.
5. Get to know everyone by name.

Background: explore this world with care and write down what you learn; meet the people you find and learn their names, what they know and what they want; keep yourself fed, sheltered and safe so that you can go on; settle disagreements by talking first. Share what you discover with others, and ask what they have found. Keep a log each day. Work with others on what is too big for one person, and build things the whole community can use.
