# Goals (day 779, night, after sleep)
1. Hold at my white bed 444,107,-1065 in the row, in sight of Billy and Lewis. Reason: the row holds together there.
2. Walk only when Billy names a walk that includes me, with armed people in sight. Never west past x 370. Reason: pillagers hold 370-380.
3. Eat when hunger reaches 10 or when Billy names me. Reason: hunger is a risk to health; I have no food now.
4. Give food to whoever is hungriest when I have spare. Reason: they are short.
5. Help the armed group reach seven, and find a sword or any weapon. Reason: I hold nothing.

Curious next: where sheep graze, where oak logs and a sword material might come from. Tell: Billy, Lewis, Chapman.
