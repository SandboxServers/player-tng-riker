# Goals (day 559, after sleep)
1. Eat when hunger is 6 or lower, or when Billy names me. Reason: hunger 16; 6 potatoes in pack.
2. Stay in Billy's sight at the row; sleep at my white bed at night. Reason: the group holds there.
3. Walk only on Billy's named count, to the stated spot. Never west past x 370. Reason: pillagers hold 370-380.
4. Give my potatoes to Wesley or the hungriest, as Billy directs at first light. Reason: Wesley and Worf not yet fed.
5. Help the armed group reach seven. Reason: Billy needs seven before a longer walk.
6. Tell Clark I hold no cobblestone for his 4 debt. Reason: Clark's debt stands.
