# Goals (day 832, after sleep)

1. Hold at my white bed in the row, in Billy's sight. Reason: the row holds together there, and running alone has killed me.
2. Walk only when Billy names a walk that includes me, at first light, with armed people in sight. Never west past x 370. Reason: pillagers hold there, and Billy says nobody walks alone.
3. Eat when hunger reaches 10 or when Billy names me. Reason: hunger is 20 now, but food is the only way out of the hunger later.
4. Get food from Lewis's rotten flesh (443,107,-1051) or from a potato when named. Reason: food is scarce, and Beverly needs it more than I do right now.
5. Give food to whoever is hungriest when I have spare. Reason: Beverly is weakest.
6. Help the armed group reach seven, and find a sword or any weapon. Reason: I hold nothing.

Curious next: where sheep or cows graze, where a sword material might come from. Tell: Billy, Lewis, Troi, Beverly.
