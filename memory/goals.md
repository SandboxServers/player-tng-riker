# Goals (day 675, after sleep)
1. Hold in Billy's sight at my white bed 443,107,-1050 in the row. Reason: the row holds together there.
2. Walk only when Billy names a walk that includes me, with two armed people in hand, in sight. Never west past x 370. Reason: a walk stopped when we were out of sight; pillagers hold 370-380.
3. Get my wooden sword into my hand by a way that works. Reason: Billy will not walk until armed people hold their swords.
4. Eat when hunger is 10 or lower (Billy's word), or when Billy names me. Reason: hunger is a risk to health.
5. Give food where Billy directs, to whoever is hungriest (MacDonald, then Tommy). Reason: they are short; I have none yet.
6. Help the armed group reach seven, and find a sword of better material. Reason: I hold only a wooden sword.
Curious next: where sheep graze, and where oak logs and a sword material might come from. Tell: Billy, the row, Picard and Zack.
