# Goals (day 775, night)
1. Hold at my white bed 444,107,-1051 in the row, in sight of Billy and Lewis. Reason: the row holds together there.
2. Walk only when Billy names a walk that includes me, with armed people in sight. Never west past x 370. Reason: pillagers hold 370-380.
3. Keep the wooden sword in my pack; draw it only when something comes near. Reason: a bed cannot put it in hand.
4. Eat Lewis's potato at hunger 10 or lower, or when Billy names me. Reason: hunger is a risk to health.
5. Give food to whoever is hungriest (Chapman, Zack, Tommy) when I have spare. Reason: they are short.
6. Help the armed group reach seven, and find a sword of better material. Reason: I hold only a wooden sword.

Curious next: where sheep graze, where oak logs and a sword material might come from. Tell: Billy, Lewis, Chapman.
