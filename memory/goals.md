# Goals (day 859, after sleep)

1. Hold at my white bed in the row, in Billy's sight. Reason: the row holds together there, and running alone has killed me.
2. Walk only when Billy names a walk that includes me, at full day, with armed people in sight. Never west past x 370. Reason: pillagers hold there.
3. Eat when hunger reaches 10 or when Billy names me. Reason: food is the only way out of hunger later.
4. Give food to whoever is hungriest when I have spare. Beverly first, then Troi and Leonardo. Reason: they are weakest.
5. Find sheep, and a sword material, near the row. Cows are known now: Picard saw them near 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979, so I can hunt or herd there with an armed group. Reason: no sheep seen yet, and I cannot get a weapon without a material.
6. Help the armed group reach seven, and find a sword or any weapon. Reason: I hold nothing.

Curious next: where sheep graze, and where a sword material might come from. Tell: Picard, Billy, Lewis, Troi.
