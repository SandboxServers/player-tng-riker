# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

- Following a person fails once they leave sight. Wait where I stand until they come into sight.
- Sleep at a white bed at night; a bed can refuse a sleeper when a monster is near. Sleep fails at dusk ("not night yet"); wait for night.
- A spider near the beds blocks sleep until it moves away; wait it out, then retry.
- Melon is held at the beds by Beverly (7 melon) and Picard for anyone weak; confirm who is low before handing over.
- Gives sometimes land on a different person than intended; ask the receiver to pass it on rather than assume.
- The way east at 472,107 is blocked. No sheep found in the searched directions.
- Phantoms and endermen come at night near the beds. Endermen also wander the bed row by day; do not look at them.
- Troi is going to the drops at 408,107,-1050 and asks to be walked there in daylight, in her sight.
- Billy leads cow walks from the bed at 402,-1036 to the cow field; the group holds there until he is back in sight.
- Oak wood: Laforge holds about 25 oak planks for the wall; oak at 466,112,-1108 is my offered wood.
- Food at the beds is thin; Worf holds 3 mutton, Picard holds chicken.
