# Beliefs

- The row at 443,107,-1051 holds together; stay by my bed in Billy's sight.
- Pillagers hold west of x 370 and the south path. Never go there.
- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low. Eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it.
- Billy names walks. No walk means nobody walks.
- Beds in the row are white beds; sleeping in one works.
- Day 782: no sheep or oak seen by anyone in the row yet. No food or weapon in the row.
- Day 783: still no verified route west and no sheep seen past the row. Billy's group count is ten in the row.
- Day 786: still no walk, no route, no sheep, no pillager news. Picard has no food to give.
- Day 788: same. No walk named, no sheep, no route west, no pillager news from anyone. Nobody at hunger 10 or below yet. Dusk is a sign to sleep in bed.

- Day 790: still no walk, sheep, route west or pillager news. Johnny has no sword either. Billy says food is thin and will name a walk with escorts once a sheep or cow is seen.
