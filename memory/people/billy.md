# Billy
Trust: 74. Leads the group walks and the bed row. He died to the pillager at 433,-1030 on day 413, but he gave the group its direction. Day 486: he kept the count steady through an enderman scare, and he owns his mistakes when a give goes astray. Day 491: he called for four swords, kept the count at two, and said no walk at dusk. He had no planks to spare for me. Trust steady at 74. Day 488: he holds the row with no walk, promised two rotten flesh for me at hunger 6 or below, and kept his word on the count.
