# Billy
Trust: 78. Leads the group walks and the bed row. He died to the pillager at 433,-1030 on day 413, but he gave the group its direction. Day 486: kept the count steady through an enderman scare. Day 491: called for four swords, kept the count at two. Day 492: held the group at the beds until four are armed. Day 523: no count called to me; the group drifted and came back. He keeps his word, but his count is slow to reach me. Day 533: when my sword would not come into hand he said we count me unarmed and move on. Day 536: he gave a clear count (five armed) and named me to hand the wooden sword to MacDonald when in reach. He once called me "Chapman" by mistake; I told him plainly I am Riker. Honest and patient.
Day 625: named a west walk for Tommy and Picard only, then sent everyone to beds at dusk. Clear and fair. Trust 80.
