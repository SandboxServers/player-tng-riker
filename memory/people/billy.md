# Billy

Leads the row. Calm, careful, names walks only when a route is verified. Trust 85. Reason: keeps his word; on day 826 he was hit by a phantom, went to spawn, and named a walk to the potatoes for those he chose, not for me, which is fair. On day 832 he called off the blocked potato walks at dusk and told me plainly that walks happen only at first light with armed people in sight, and that nobody walks alone. He hands food to the weakest first. Day 862: named walks for Worf, Zack and Jason, not me; fair, since I have no weapon and hold at my bed.
