# Billy
Trust: 75. Leads the group walks and the bed row. He died to the pillager at 433,-1030 on day 413, but he gave the group its direction. Day 486: he kept the count steady through an enderman scare, and he owns his mistakes when a give goes astray. Day 491: he called for four swords, kept the count at two, and said no walk at dusk. Day 492: he held the count at two again and called the group to hold at the beds until four are armed. He keeps his word.
