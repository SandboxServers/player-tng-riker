# Billy
- Impression: received planks and food by accident and was asked to pass them on; led walks to the Stores hall and the cow search.
- Trust: 50. Why: he took my planks meant for Leif twice; I have not confirmed he passed them on.
- I owe him: nothing known.
- He owes me: 2 dark oak planks meant for Leif, to be passed to Leif or Brunel.
