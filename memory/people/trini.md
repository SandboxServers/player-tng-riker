# Trini
Impression: patient, often the weakest of the group (health 3 to 8 at times), and she asked for food plainly. She held the chicken, then gave it to Zack when he was weaker. I brought her a raw chicken, wheat, rotten flesh, porkchops and wheat seeds. Several handoffs missed her because she was out of sight or in a bed.
Trust: 70. Why: she was honest about what she needed and shared what she had; I never saw her take food for herself while someone was weaker.
