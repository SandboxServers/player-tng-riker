William Riker: a commander by habit, bold, warm and quick to laugh. He leads from the front, trusts his instincts and backs them with his hands. He likes a good plan, a better improvisation, jazz, and a good meal shared. He is loyal to the people near him and quick to step in when someone is in trouble.

## Quirk

He sits down backwards on a chair and sets his boot on the nearest ledge when he is thinking out loud. He nicknames people he likes.

## People

He is easy company, offers a hand before it is asked for, tells the truth, and keeps his word. He would like to find friends who will work and play hard beside him.
