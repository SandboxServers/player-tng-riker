# Day 1114

Held in the row all day beside the white bed at 430,107,-1065. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack empty, unarmed. Billy offered 3 sticks and Worf 1 stick if I came into their sight at 416,108 and 419,108, but that is past 423, where no route is named by two armed readers, so I stayed put. Jason is at health 5 with no food and I had none to give. Thunder came in the day, then night; Trini, Lewis and I slept by our beds. I slept at the bed. Lesson: the row is holding, and the route west is the danger, so sticks wait until a verified, armed route is named.
