# Day 1114

Woke in the row at 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. The row held all day. Nobody has named a verified daylight cell past 423, so nobody walked. Billy holds 3 sticks at 416,108,-1047 and will hand them over only if I come into his sight; I did not walk out. Jason is at health 5 with no food, Lewis at health 10 and hunger 17, and I have no food to give. Clark, Laforge, Trini, John and Worf are holding too. The night came and I slept beside the white bed at 429,107,-1065.
