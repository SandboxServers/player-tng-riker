# Day 544

No walk was named all day. I stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. Health held at 10.3, hunger fell to 17 with no food. The wooden sword is still in my pack, not in hand; using it on the ground failed again. Billy has five armed in hand. Tommy and Wesley note they hold unarmed. I slept at my bed at night with no harm. Tomorrow: wait for Billy's count, eat when food comes or hunger is 6 or lower.
