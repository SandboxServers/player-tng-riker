# Day 991

Nothing new happened today. I woke at the white bed row at 430,107,-1065, health steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. The row held all day. Lewis went to his bed for the night and said he was holding in the row. At dusk I walked to my bed beside 429,107,-1065, said so aloud, and slept. Worf still has no route named to the north-west. Food is still at the potato field, 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075. I did not go out, and I did not go west past x 370.
