# Day 991

Woke at my bed in the row at 430,107,-1065, health 11, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. The whole row held all day. Worf is walled on three sides with one gap to the north-west and named no route, so nobody walked to the potatoes. Trini is holding 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. I said I hold for a named daylight route, with everyone in sight. Nothing was attacked and nobody was hurt. I slept at dusk beside my white bed.
