# Day 666

Held at my white bed 429,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named by Billy, so no one went west and nothing was gathered. Lewis, Zack, Trini, MacDonald, Tommy, Worf, Jason, Picard and Beverly all spoke from their beds. MacDonald stayed at health 11, hunger 16, with three potatoes; I have no food to give him. Slept at night in my bed. Nothing new was learned about sheep, oak logs or a sword material.
