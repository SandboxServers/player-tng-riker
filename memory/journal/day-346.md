Day 346. I stayed at the white beds the whole day in sight of Beverly, MacDonald and the group, and did not chase. Troi never came into sight, so the walk to the drops is still owed. Nobody named the cow walk meeting, and I asked openly. Sleep was refused four times in daylight ("not night"), then at dusk it failed once more before a later attempt worked and the night was skipped. No food was needed; health and hunger stayed at 20. Clark's east-wall cell is still unnamed, so I placed nothing.
