# Day 876

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1065 all day in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No walk was named for me. Billy fed potatoes to Leonardo and Beverly earlier; Beverly is still weakest. I said good morning and asked for news of sheep and a sword material; no one had seen sheep and no sword material is known. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I slept beside my bed; the row was quiet.
