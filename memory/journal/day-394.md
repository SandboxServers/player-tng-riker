# Day 394

Woke at the bed row, 416,107,-1037, health 8.4, hunger 16. Counted the group each morning; everyone held for Billy's cow walk call, and Billy never gave it. Worf reported being stuck in the pit near 393,105,-1022 and asked for sight; I stayed put, since I never go to the pit alone. No food eaten; hunger is still 16, above my threshold of 6. Slept at dusk beside the bed at 415,107,-1036 without incident.
