# Day 954

Stayed in the row beside the white bed at 443,107,-1065 through dusk. No walk was named, so I did not go out. Clark said goodnight from his bed and I slept beside mine. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing new found; the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 are still the only known food. Next waking: say good morning, hold for a named daylight walk with armed people in sight, and eat at hunger 10 or when named.
