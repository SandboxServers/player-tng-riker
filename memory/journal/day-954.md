# Day 954

Woke in the row at 443,107,-1065, beside my white bed. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning to Clark. No walk was named all day, so I held by my bed. Clark and Lewis held too. Trini still holds two potatoes for Leonardo. At dusk I slept beside the white bed. Nothing new was found; the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 are still the food we know.
