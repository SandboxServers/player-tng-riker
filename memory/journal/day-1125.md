# Day 1125

I held in the row all day. No daylight route past 423 was named, so I did not walk. Worf still holds my one stick; I went toward him in the morning but he was not in sight and the way was blocked, so I went back to the white bed at 430,107,-1064 and waited. Worf said he would bring the stick at light, in sight. Health and hunger stayed full. Jason, Johnny and Trini are still hungry with no food, and I have none to give. I slept at the white bed at night, as the rule says.
