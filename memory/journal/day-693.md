# Day 693

Held in the row all day beside the white bed at 416,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Billy named no walk. Laforge and Tommy held at their beds too; Tommy at health 15, hunger 16, with 3 potatoes. Nobody has seen sheep or oak logs near the row; cows were reported near 339,104,-979. Went to bed at dusk in the bed beside me, slept, and woke to the next day with nothing changed.
