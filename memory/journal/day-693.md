# Day 693

Held by the white bed in the row at 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes) and Laforge (health 20, hunger 20) held at their beds too. Bow drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came near. Went to bed at dusk in sight of the group.
