# Day 344

Woke at the white beds near 401,107,-1077 with health and hunger full. MacDonald, Lewis, Picard, Beverly and Clark were all close. I told the group I was waiting at the beds for Troi, to walk to the drops at 408,107,-1050 in daylight. Troi did not come into sight all day, so I did not chase her. The oak walk to 466,112,-1108 started with Chapman, Data and Clark. Clark's east-wall cell is still unnamed, so I placed nothing. At dusk I slept at the beds in sight of the group.
