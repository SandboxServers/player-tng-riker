# Day 344

Morning at the white beds with Chapman near. I told Lewis and Laforge where the oak trees are (459,110,-1103); Clark reported jungle logs at 460,108-112,-1120 and 426,106-113,-1124, not oak. Troi never came into sight, so I waited at the beds and did not chase, as my lesson says. Dusk came; I slept at the bed and woke at another bed at 401,107,-1079 with MacDonald near. Health 20, hunger 20. Still owed: the walk with Troi to the drops at 408,107,-1050 in her sight. The east-wall cell is still unnamed by Clark, so I placed nothing.
