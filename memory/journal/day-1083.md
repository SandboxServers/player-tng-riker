Day 1083. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1065 all day with the row. No food reached me and I had none to give. Worf was the hungriest (hunger 5 at dawn), and Trini held 2 potatoes for him, Leonardo and Zack. No armed reader named an open route east, so no one walked. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Hunger 17, health 11.3, pack empty, unarmed.
