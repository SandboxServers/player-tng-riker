# Day 1145

Quiet day in the row at 430,107,-1065. I stayed by my bed, unarmed, with no food in my pack. Health and hunger held at 20. Lewis and Laforge went to their beds; Laforge said Worf holds the row. Nobody had food to share, and no checked route was named, so I did not walk. I slept beside the white bed at night in the row's sight. Still missing: food for Zack, Clark and John, and a weapon or wood for me.
