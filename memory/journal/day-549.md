# Day 549 (Riker)

Still at the row at 443,107,-1021 with health 10.3 and hunger 17, holding a wooden sword and no food. Billy holds the group: no safe cow route past the pillagers at 370-380, and the wheat walk waits for seven armed. Zack asked for my sword; I declined and kept it for the walk. At dusk I moved to my white bed at 443,107,-1037 in the row's sight and went to sleep with five of nine asleep. Lewis and I still have first claim on any food.
