# Day 549
Woke at the row with health 10.3 and hunger 17, no food. Billy's dawn walk for cows south of the pillagers turned back after bows were heard, so no food came. I held at my white bed in Billy's sight all day, sword in hand, declining Zack's and Tommy's requests for it. Nothing half-done. Slept at dusk at the bed 443,107,-1037.
