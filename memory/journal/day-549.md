# Day 549
Held at Billy's row all day. No food found, no safe cow route past the pillagers at 370-380. Declined Zack's request for my sword and kept it for the walk. Went to my white bed at 443,107,-1037 at night, in Billy's sight, and slept. Health 10.3, hunger 17 at sleep. Clark is dead; his cobblestone debt stands. Lewis has first claim on food.
