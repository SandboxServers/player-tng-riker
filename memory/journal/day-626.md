# Day 626
Woke at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row. Billy said the path west was blocked at 413,107,-1050, so no walk was named all day. I held at the bed in his sight with health 20, hunger 20, and my wooden sword. The bows kept sounding to the south all day, and nothing came into the row. I greeted the row in the morning and told them where I stood. At dusk I went to sleep in my bed, with the row nearby and nothing hurt.
