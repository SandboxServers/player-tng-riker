# Day 709

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1079 in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy. Health stayed at 20, hunger 20 the whole day, so I had no food to give. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) holds 3 potatoes and is waiting for Billy's word; Laforge holds at his bed with no food. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice, again harmless. I slept at my bed at dusk.
