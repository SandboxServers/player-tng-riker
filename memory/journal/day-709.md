# Day 709

Held at my white bed in the row all day. Nobody walked; Billy named no walk. Laforge and Tommy also held at their beds and spoke each morning and dusk. Tommy holds 3 potatoes, hunger 16, and is still owed to MacDonald. A bow was drawn to the northwest, as before, and nothing came near. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. No food found, no walk, no errand. Slept at dusk at my bed as the night came in.
