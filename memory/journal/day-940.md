# Day 940

Quiet day in the row. No walk was named, no one spoke, and nothing was gathered. I stayed by the white bed at 443,107,-1065 with my health steady at 11.3 and my hunger at 17, unarmed and with an empty pack. At night thunder came over the row, and I went to sleep beside the bed. Food is still the thing to fix: the ripe potatoes are at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, and Trini holds two for Leonardo.
