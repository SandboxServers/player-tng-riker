Day 940. Slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1065 in the row. Woke at day to Clark, Trini and Lewis holding for a named daylight walk; none was named. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo (health 1), then Worf. I have no food and no weapon, so I could only say so and hold. Health steady at 11.3, hunger 17.
