# Day 752

Held at my white bed in the row all day, at 416,107,-1079. Health and hunger stayed at 20. No walk was named by Billy. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came near. Tommy holds 3 potatoes at hunger 16 and Laforge holds no food. Laforge and Tommy both spoke at dusk and dawn. I spoke once, to say I hold and will sleep at night. I went to bed at night in sight of the row.
