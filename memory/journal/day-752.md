# Day 752

Held at my white bed in the row all day at 416,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. No walk was named by Billy, and none was taken. A bow was heard drawn to the northwest once; nothing came near. Tommy (3 potatoes, hunger 16) and Laforge (no food, hunger 20) are holding at their beds and speak at dawn and dusk. Laforge reports no verified west route. Tommy is waiting on Billy for any walk or give. I went to bed at night in the row, sheltered and in sight of the others. Next: hold at bed at waking, wait for Billy's named walk, never west past x 370, give food to Tommy first when Billy directs.
