# Day 734

Held at the white bed in the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Opened the chest by the bed (419,107,-1079); my readings showed no contents. Billy named no walk. Tommy and Laforge held at their beds with no food to spare; Tommy keeps his 3 potatoes until Billy calls. A bow was drawn to the northwest again; nothing came near. Slept at the bed at dusk.
