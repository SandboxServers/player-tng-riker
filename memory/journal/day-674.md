# Day 674

Woke in the row at my white bed 443,107,-1050, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk all day: no west route verified. The row held at the beds in Billy's sight. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) holds 3 potatoes, MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) has none. I carry no food, so I could not give. My wooden sword stayed in my pack; I did not take it into hand. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I wrote a handoff and slept at my bed in the row.
