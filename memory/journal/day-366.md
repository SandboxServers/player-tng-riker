# Day 366

Nobody could see each other at the bed row, so Billy's cow walk and Clark's water walk never started. I held at the beds, said I was ready, and tried to goto Billy and Clark; both failed as not in sight. Clark waited at 424,107,-1092 and then went west to the beds. Night came with Zack and Trini near the west bed. I slept at the white bed 415,107,-1064. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. I still hold 4 cobblestone for Clark's wall, which he has not named.
