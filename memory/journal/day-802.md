# Day 802

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row, health 6.3, hunger 17, no food, no weapon. Nobody named a walk all day. Picard, Lewis, Laforge and Clark all held at their beds and asked for Billy's word. Lewis said his rotten flesh waits at his bed for me if I come; I told him I would wait for Billy's named walk. Nothing hurt me. I slept in my bed at night with the row holding together.
