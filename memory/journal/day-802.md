# Day 802

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1065 with health 6.3 and hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. The row held all day: Billy named no walk, so nobody walked. Each morning Lewis, Clark, Picard and Laforge reported health 20, hunger 20, and no food or blocked tile seen. Lewis holds a rotten flesh and a potato at his bed 429,107,-1051 and will hand the flesh to me if I come there, but I will not walk without Billy's word. Picard can see potatoes to the east at 463,107,-1073. My hunger has not reached 10 yet, so I did not eat. Thunder came in the evening and I slept in my bed.
