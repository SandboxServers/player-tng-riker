Day 965. Woke in the row at my white bed, hunger 17, health 11.3, pack empty. Said good morning to Clark. Lewis and Clark both held at their beds with no food to share, and no walk was named by Worf or Billy, so nobody went to the potatoes. Dusk came and I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1065. Nothing new was found; sword material and sheep are still unknown.
