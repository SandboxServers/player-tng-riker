# Day 681

Woke at the row by the beds at 416,107,-1079, alone, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody came; no walk was named by Billy, who has not been seen since day 676. Heard a bow drawn to the northwest and kept to the beds. At dusk I tried to use the bed directly, which failed ("that block is a bed"), then sleep worked and I am asleep with 2 of 10 needed. Nothing new learned except that a bed is slept in by sleep, not by use.
