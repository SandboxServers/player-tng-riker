# Day 792

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Held by the bed all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named. Picard, Laforge, MacDonald and Lewis all hold no food, and the row has none. Nothing new was learned about routes, sheep or weapons. Went to bed at dusk in my own bed.
