# Day 792

Woke at my bed area, walked to my white bed 443,107,-1065 and held there with the row. Jason, Lewis, MacDonald, Picard and LaForge all holding, armed and waiting for Billy. No walk was named all day. Picard reports ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line; I said I would carry food back if Billy names the walk. Hunger stayed at 17 and I have no food. Health steady at 6.3. Went to sleep in my bed at night.
