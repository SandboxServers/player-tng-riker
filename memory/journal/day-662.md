Day 662. Dusk at my white bed 429,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword. No walk named all day; the row held in sight. MacDonald (health 11) and Tommy (health 15) are short of food. Zack holds 4 potatoes and offered them to Worf, who would not take one without Billy's word. I said I hold in the row and no walk is named. Slept in my bed; nothing new came of it.
