# Day 998

Woke at my white bed in the row, 430,107,-1065. Health about 11, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing new came in: Zack spoke from the row, holding with no walk until a named daylight route. I held still, said so aloud, and slept at the bed as night came. No errand out, no one hurt. Still waiting on Worf or Billy for a route to the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075. Never west past x 370.
