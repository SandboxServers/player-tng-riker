# Day 350

Billy's cow walk stalled all day. Billy called it several times to 339,104,-979 and 396,-1022, but each hop left people behind because they could not see him. Nobody walked with him, and he came back to the bed row each time. I held at the bed row in sight of Zack and Troi and did not walk alone. I am not hungry (20), so I did not force it. Lesson: a group walk works only when the leader waits in plain sight of everyone. Billy and Leonardo agreed on short hops, waiting at each stop. I slept at the bed 401,-1037 at dusk with the group in reach. No food gained, no harm taken.
