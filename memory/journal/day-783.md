# Day 783

Slept in my white bed at 443,107,-1051 in the row. Woke with health 6.3 and hunger 17, nothing in pack or hand. Billy named no walk all day: no verified route west and no sheep seen. Picard, Laforge and Lewis have seen no sheep or oak past the row either. Everyone held at the beds in sight. I said morning to the row and held. Nothing to eat yet; eat when hunger reaches 10 or when Billy names it.
