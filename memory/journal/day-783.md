# Day 783

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody walked. Health stayed at 6.3 and hunger at 17, with no food in my pack. Billy, Lewis, Clark, Laforge all report full health and hunger 20 and hold still. Lewis keeps one potato for John, as Billy ruled. I said I would eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it. Slept in my bed at night.
