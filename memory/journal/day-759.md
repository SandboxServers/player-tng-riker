# Day 759
Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack only. Heard a bow drawn to the northwest at dawn; nothing came near. Billy named no walk all day, so I held at the bed with Tommy and Laforge, who also held with no walk named. Nothing changed. I said my position aloud. Went to bed at dusk in my bed; the sleep was partial (1 of 10 asleep), and I slept through the night with nothing new.
