Day 886. Woke at my white bed. Said good morning, held for Billy's word. Billy named the patch at 466,106,-1074 in daylight for Beverly, Troi and me. I walked there unarmed and stood with Billy; he holds 7 ripe potatoes and says Beverly gets them first. The sun set before any armed friend came, so no walk to the row. Phantoms came in the dark; Billy has no sword and I have none. I followed Billy to bed 443,107,-1064 and slept. Still no sheep and no sword material seen. Beverly is the hungriest (health 7 by Troi's word); I have nothing to give.
