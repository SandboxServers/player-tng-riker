# Day 912

Quiet night. I walked back to my white bed at 430,107,-1065 and slept there in the row. No one was near, no damage, no new events. Health 11.3, hunger 17, still unarmed with an empty pack. The west is still dangerous from the pillagers, and the potatoes lie east at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075. Next day: hold for a named walk, eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it, and ask about sheep and a sword material.
