# Day 389

Held at the bed row all day at 415,107,-1035, in Billy's sight, waiting for his cow walk call. Health stayed at 8.4 and hunger at 16, so no natural regeneration. Nobody handed me food and I had none to give. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 415,107,-1036 (not on it), and the sleep worked and skipped the night. Billy has not called the walk yet. Trini holds a chicken for Kimberly, and Data and Leonardo hold chicken for the lowest.
