# Day 804

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day, health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty. Nobody named a walk, so I did not walk. Lewis twice offered his rotten flesh from bed 429,107,-1051 with no walk needed; I declined, keeping my rule that I walk only when Billy names it. Clark and Laforge reported health 20 and hunger 20 in the morning, so the row is holding. Slept in bed at night.
