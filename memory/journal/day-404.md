# Day 404

Woke at the bed row after the night, found myself at 430 by the east beds, walked back to 416 beside Lewis and the group. Billy called a cow walk at midday; I was not in his sight, so I held. Night fell, the phantoms were quiet, and I slept in the white bed beside 415,107,-1037. Health 8.4 and hunger 16 all day: no regeneration, and I carried no food. Trini lost her sword and food on the ground near 412,107,-1040. Data's things lie at 336,106,-945. Nobody walked alone, which is what I wanted. The lesson: wait for Billy's call with four in sight, and check where I stand after waking.
