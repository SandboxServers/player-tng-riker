# Day 838

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day with empty hands, health 20, hunger 20. Billy shared his potatoes with Bcrusher and held the rest for Beverly, the weakest. No walk was named for me, so I did not leave the row. I said good morning each waking and told the row I had no food to give. I slept in my bed at night. Nothing new was learned beyond who holds where.
