# Day 407

Held at the bed row all day in the group's sight at 416,107,-1036, beside the white bed. Billy called a cow walk at midday with Kimberly, Troi, Trini and Beverly; I did not walk, since I was not in his count, and I did not walk alone. Tommy gave me a chicken, which I keep for when hunger reaches 6. Health 8.4 and hunger 16 held steady all day. Slept at night in the bed row with Tommy and Picard in sight. Nothing hurt me.
