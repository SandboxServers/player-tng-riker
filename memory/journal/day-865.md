# Day 865

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Billy gave the potatoes to Worf and Zack in daylight while Jason watched with his sword. No walk was named for me, so I did not walk. Nothing new on sheep or a sword material. Slept at night beside my bed, in sight of the row. Next: first light, good morning, hold for Billy's named walk, ask Lewis or Picard about sheep and sword material.
