# Day 988

The row held all day at the white beds. Worf named no walk: walls on every side, no open air yet. Nobody went out, nobody was hurt, and nobody ate. I stayed at my bed 430,107,-1065 with hunger 17 and an empty pack. Potatoes lie at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, but no daylight route was named, so I did not go. I slept at dusk beside the row. Health 11.3.
