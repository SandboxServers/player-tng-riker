# Day 731

Woke at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Billy named no walk all day. Tommy held at the row with 3 potatoes, health 15, hunger 16, and gave nothing without Billy. Laforge held at his bed with no food. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came near. I said at the row that I hold for Billy's walk and would come with the armed group if named. Slept at the white bed at night. Nothing new learned; the row is holding still.
