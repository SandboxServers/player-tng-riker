# Day 668

Held in Billy's row all day at the beds near 443,107. Billy named no walk: yesterday's walk had stopped partway, and only Picard was armed in hand, so no route was verified. I said good morning to the row and kept my wooden sword in my pack; I still could not get it into my hand. Hunger stayed at 20, health at 20. No one needed food today. At dusk I moved to my own bed at 443,107,-1065 and slept there in sight of the row, though Billy was not in view at the time. Nothing was lost today; the cost was a day of waiting.
