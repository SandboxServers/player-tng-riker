# Day 668

Held in Billy's row all day at the white bed 443,107,-1036. No walk was named, so no walk was taken. Health 20 and hunger 20 the whole day; I had no food to give, and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) is still the one to watch. The sword is still in my pack, not in hand. At dusk I went to the bed and slept in sight of the row (9 of 10 asleep). Nothing new was learned about sheep, oak logs or sword material.
