# Day 536

Woke at the white bed 444,107,-1037 beside Billy's row, unarmed, hunger 17, no food held. No one near at first. Billy's speech called me "Chapman" about the wooden sword; I asked him whether to hand it to MacDonald. Billy later named me: give the sword to MacDonald when he is in reach, else hold it for Laforge after me. Count at day 536: Billy, Picard, Worf, Jason and MacDonald armed. No walk was named, so I held. Laforge and Worf are holding at their beds in Billy's sight. Worf's goto to his bed was blocked; Billy sent him to the bed at 429,107,-1036. At night I slept at my bed; the sleep worked on the second try. Nothing was gained today except a clearer count of who holds what.
