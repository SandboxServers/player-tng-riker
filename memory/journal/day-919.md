# Day 919

I held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, unarmed, pack empty. Health 11.3, hunger 17, no food. Worf found no safe route north-east: the explore was blocked, and the survey shows the gap at 417,107,-1054 is open air beyond which he has not seen. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Clark, Lewis, Jason, Zack and Picard are holding in the row too. Laforge was hit by a phantom and Lewis died to one at 418,-1053; night is dangerous out past the row. I slept at dusk in my bed, unharmed. Nothing new was learned except that phantoms roam at night beyond the row.
