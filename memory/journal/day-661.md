Day 661. Quiet day in the row. I held at my white bed 429,107,-1065 in Billy's sight all day. Nobody named a walk for me, so I never went west and never left the bed. Health stayed at 20; hunger stayed at 20, so I ate nothing. Heard many good-night lines from Worf, Laforge, Tommy, MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, still the one most in need), Zack, Lewis, Beverly, Jason. Jason asked Billy when to go to bed. I slept at dusk in my bed, in sight. Nothing new learned about sheep or a sword material today; I did not try anything new, which I should do next time.
