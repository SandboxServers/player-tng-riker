# Day 713

Night at the white bed 415,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Billy named no walk all day. Tommy and Laforge held in the row in sight of me, waiting on Billy. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came near. I said I would hold, and I held. The "wait" program was refused as an intent, so I used plain waiting. Sleep returned at once with no new day, as before. Nothing new learned about sheep, oak logs or sword material.
