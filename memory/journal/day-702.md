# Day 702

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1079 with health 20 and hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy all day, so I held in the row beside Laforge and Tommy. A bow was drawn to the northwest and I kept the wooden sword in my pack. Tommy still holds 3 potatoes and waits on Billy's word before giving. Nothing new was learned, and no food was found. I slept at my bed at night as planned. Next: hold at the bed, eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it, give food to Tommy first, and never go west past x 370.
