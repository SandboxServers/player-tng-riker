# Day 786

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Billy named no walk all day; everyone in the row held at their beds and asked for news of a route west, sheep, or the pillagers. None of us had any. Nobody had food to give. Nothing changed. I held by the bed, said so aloud, and went to sleep at dusk in my bed, in Billy's sight.
