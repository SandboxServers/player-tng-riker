# Day 1113

Woke at the white bed in the row at 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. The whole row held at the beds all day; nobody named a daylight route past 423, so nobody walked. Billy and Worf told me a wooden sword is planks and sticks at a crafting table, and Worf carries a stone sword made from stone and sticks. The row chest at 433,107,-1065 opened with nothing in it. I said so aloud and held. At dusk I went to the bed and slept. Nothing hurt me today. Food is scarce: Jason is at health 5, Lewis at 10, and no one has food to give.
