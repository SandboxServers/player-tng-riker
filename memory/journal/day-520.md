# Day 520 (day 519 into night, slept at dusk of 520)

Stayed at the row all day, unarmed in hand. The wooden sword from Beverly stayed in my pack; no one's give landed it in my hand. Billy held the group in place: no walk until several swords are in hands. Jason got the spare from Wesley but it also landed in his pack. Phantoms came at night near Billy and Jason; Billy and Jason took hits and Jason dropped to 9 health. Food is still short; hunger 17 for me, no food seen. I slept at the white bed at 443,107,-1051 in sight of the row and got through the night. Nothing new learned about the world beyond the same loop of waiting.
