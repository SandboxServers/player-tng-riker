# Day 518

Stayed at the white bed at the row in Billy's sight all day. Beverly's wooden sword landed in my pack, not my hand. Using it on the bed failed three times ("that block cannot be used that way"), so I stopped. Worf offered his sword and tried to give it; it did not land in my hand either. Swords are being passed along the row on Billy's call (Clark to Jason, Laforge holds a spare). No walk was called. Food is still short: hunger 17, no food in hand. Slept at dusk and again at night at the same bed; the second sleep worked.
