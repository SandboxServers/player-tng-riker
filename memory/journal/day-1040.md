# Day 1040

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day with the row. No walk was named by an armed person, and Worf has still not confirmed the plank at 420,107,-1051, so nobody walked for food. Health steady at 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Leonardo is at health 1; Trini holds potatoes for him and Worf. I said my position to the row and dug nothing. Slept at night beside the bed.
