# Day 1062

Dusk at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row. Hunger 17, health 11, pack empty, unarmed. No open cell past the planks was named by anyone, so no walk. Said goodnight to the row and slept in my bed. Waking: still holding. The row says the same: Billy, Zack, Jason, Lewis all report planks east and no route to the potatoes. Next light: hold, eat at hunger 10 or when named, walk only on a route named by two armed readers.
