# Day 730

Held at the white bed in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20, no food; the wooden sword stayed in my pack. Tommy (3 potatoes, health 15, hunger 16) and Laforge (no food) held beside me and asked the same thing: wait for Billy's call. A bow was drawn to the northwest once, and nothing came near. Nothing changed, and I slept in my bed at night.
