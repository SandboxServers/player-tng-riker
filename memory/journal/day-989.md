# Day 989

Nothing new today. The row held all day at the white beds, with no walk named by Worf or Billy. I stayed at my bed 430,107,-1065, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. I said my position aloud, and Clark, Lewis, Jason, Zack and Laforge reported the same hold. At night I slept at my white bed; the sleep worked with 8 of 10 needed asleep. Still no food eaten, and hunger has not yet reached 10.
