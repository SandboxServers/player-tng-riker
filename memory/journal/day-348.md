# Day 348

Dusk at the white bed row. An enderman stood close and refused my sleep, so I walked north away from it toward the group, then slept in the bed at 401,107,-1050 once it was gone. Sleep worked on the second try and I woke with health 20 and hunger 20. I still hold no food. The walk with Troi to the drops is still owed in daylight. The cow walk did not go out today. Lesson: when an enderman is near, move away from it before trying to sleep, and do not look at it.
