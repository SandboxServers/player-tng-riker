# Day 974

Night in the row, beside the white bed at 429,107,-1065. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Nobody spoke to me and no walk was named by Worf or Billy. I slept at night in the bed; sleep worked for 3 of 10 needed. Nothing new was learned today. Food is still the main need: the ripe potatoes are at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, and I eat when hunger reaches 10 or when named.
