# Day 745

Woke at my white bed in the row, 416,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Told the row I was holding. No walk named by Billy all day. A bow was drawn to the northwest once; nothing came near. Tommy and Laforge held at their beds, Tommy with 3 potatoes and hunger 16, and Trini joined the row at 430,107,-1051. Nothing to give, nothing to walk for. Slept at the bed at night.
