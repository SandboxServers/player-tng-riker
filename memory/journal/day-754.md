# Day 754

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day with Tommy and Laforge. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy named no walk. I asked the row whether anyone had seen sheep or oak logs; Tommy has seen none near. Tommy reports health 15 and hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes. Laforge holds no food. Nothing came near; a bow was drawn to the northwest twice. Slept at night in my bed.
