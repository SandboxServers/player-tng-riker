# Day 821

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3. Lewis still holds my rotten flesh at 443,107,-1051; I did not ask for it since hunger is above 10. Heard Troi and Worf bring a potato to Beverly, who ate it and rose to 5.3. Slept at dusk in my bed with the row in sight; partial sleep, 5 of 10 needed.
