# Day 821

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, hunger 17, health 6.3, no food, unarmed. Billy named no walk, so I did not walk to Lewis's rotten flesh at 443,107,-1051. Troi walked to Beverly with a potato; Zack gave his potato to Beverly; Worf and Lewis hold swords at the beds. I said my need aloud and held my place. Slept in my bed at dusk.
