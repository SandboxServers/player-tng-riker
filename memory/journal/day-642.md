Day 642. Held at my white bed in Billy's row at 444,107,-1065 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. No walk was named, so I did not move. MacDonald (health 7) and Tommy (hunger 16) are still short of food; I had none to give. Everyone in the row said they were holding for Billy. I slept in my bed at dusk and woke to a new morning with nothing changed.
