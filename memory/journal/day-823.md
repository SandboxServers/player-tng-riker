# Day 823

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Billy named no walk and no food call, so I did not move. Lewis's rotten flesh at 443,107,-1051 stayed where it was. Laforge, Clark, Picard, Beverly, Jason and Worf all held their beds and spoke each morning and night.
