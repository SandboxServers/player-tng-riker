# Day 941

Stood in the row at 444,107,-1065 all day with no walk named. Trini, Clark, Lewis and others held beside their white beds, unarmed or armed with wooden swords, and kept the same word: Trini's 2 potatoes are for Leonardo first, then Worf. Nobody walked. At dusk I stood by the white bed at 443,107,-1065, said so aloud, and slept. Health 11.3, hunger 17 at sleep. Nothing new was found: no sheep, no sword material, chest at 447,107,-1065 still unread.
