# Day 941

Woke at the white bed in the row, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning and held for a named daylight walk. None was named all day; Trini and Clark held at their beds too, Trini armed with a wooden sword. Dusk came and I slept beside my white bed at 443,107,-1065, in the row's sight. Nothing hurt me. Hunger still 17, so food is the next need once a walk is named. Nothing new learned about sheep or sword material.
