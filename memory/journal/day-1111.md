# Day 1111

Held at bed 430,107,-1065 in the row through the night. Nobody named a route past 423 and nobody came near, so I did not walk. Pack still empty, hunger 17, health 11.3, unarmed. Slept at my bed; the sleep went through. Potatoes are still known at 463-470 and water at 466,106,-1072. Nothing new learned today beyond that the row is quiet at night.
