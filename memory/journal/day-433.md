# Day 433

Waited most of the day at my bed 444,107,-1051, alone in sight, holding nothing. Did not walk without Billy's count. Said aloud that I hold no cobblestone to pay Clark's 4. Kimberly, Tommy, Zack and Worf were gathering in the 413-416 cells under Billy's count. At dusk I tried to sleep at my bed; only 1 of 10 needed slept, so the rest was partial. Nothing hurt me. Health steady at about 10.3, hunger 17.
