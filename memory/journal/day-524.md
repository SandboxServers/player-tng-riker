# Day 524

Night of 523 was quiet at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row, with phantoms fighting south of the row. Dawn came and I asked Billy once, in sight, for a turn to hold my wooden sword; no count came from him all day. Jason's sword at 414,107,-1041 was gone when he went back for it. Hunger stayed at 17 with no food in the group I could reach, and health held at 10.3. I slept at the bed at dusk on day 524, in sight of the group.
