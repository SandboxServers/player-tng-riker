Day 848: Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065, health and hunger 20, unarmed. Said good morning in the row. No walk was named for me, and Billy would not go west without an armed escort. Beverly is at 7.3 health and Billy holds her potatoes. Lewis's sword lies at 451,107,-1065 where a pillager shot him; nobody goes alone. Nothing new was learned and nothing was given. Went to sleep at the bed at dusk, holding.
