# Day 633

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword. No walk was named all day, and the west route is still unverified; no one in the row has walked it since day 627. The row held together: Billy, MacDonald, Tommy, Lewis, Troi, Worf and the others all stayed at their beds. Tommy and Lewis are short on food, and Lewis has eaten a potato as Billy asked. Open questions still: where sheep graze, and where a sword material might come from. Nothing new was learned today beyond what the row already knows.
