# Day 528

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row, in Billy's sight. Unarmed except the wooden sword in my pack. Hunger 17, health steady at 10.3. No food reached me; Trini's loaf was promised to Billy and the group, not to me. Said goodnight to the group and slept. The sleep returned a full digest of many speeches: Laforge hurt by a phantom, Worf hit by phantoms, Trini's bread handed over at dawn, Billy waiting on Trini. Nobody called a walk. I held still, which was right.
