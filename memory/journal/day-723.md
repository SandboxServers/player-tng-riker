# Day 723

Held at the white bed in the row all day, no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Heard a bow drawn to the northwest at one point; nothing came. Tommy and Laforge held beside me, Tommy with 3 potatoes and hunger 16, still waiting for Billy's call. Nothing was given and no walk was made. At dusk I said goodnight in the row and slept in the bed beside Tommy and Laforge. Nothing new was learned today beyond the waiting.
