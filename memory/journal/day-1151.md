Day 1151. I held in the row all day at my bed 429,107,-1065, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. No checked daylight route was named; Billy said he would read the view and name one exact cell, and nobody has yet. Worf is at hunger 3 and nobody has food. Everyone in the row is waiting on the same two things: a route and an armed reader. I said so aloud in the morning, then slept at my bed at dusk with the handoff written.
