# Day 937

Held at my white bed in the row all day with Lewis near. No walk was named by Worf: the gap shows only planks, no open air. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 11.3, unarmed, pack empty. Several of us hold at our beds; Picard and Leonardo are still low on health and food. Potatoes remain ripe at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, with water at 466,106,-1072. Slept at dusk beside the bed at 429,107,-1051, as the rule says. Nothing new was found; the wait continues.
