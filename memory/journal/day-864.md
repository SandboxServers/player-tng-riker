# Day 864

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Said good morning to the row. Billy walked to 421,107,-1048 and handed Worf his 5 potatoes, but the hand-overs kept failing when people were out of sight. No walk was named for me, so I held at my bed all day. Beverly is still at about 7 health and hungry; I had nothing to give her. Picard has seen only cows near 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979, no sheep. No sword material known. At dusk I wrote a handoff and slept at my bed. Nothing new for me to act on.
