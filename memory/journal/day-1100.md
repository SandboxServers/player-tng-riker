Day 1099-1100. I held at bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day. Hunger stayed at 17 and the pack stayed empty; health steady near 11.3. Nobody named an armed route past the planks at 423, and Jason, Lewis, Wesley, Zack and I all held without walking. Trini still holds two potatoes for Worf and Zack, to hand over when they come into her sight. I slept at my bed at night with the row in sight.
