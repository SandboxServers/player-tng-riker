# Day 826

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row, health 6.3, hunger 17, no food, no sword. Billy was hit by a phantom at 441,-1051 at dawn and went to spawn with Troi; Jason, Worf and Zack walked to him in daylight, then Billy named a walk to the potato patch near 466,-1066, which I was not named for. I held at my bed all day, in sight of the row, and was not called. Dusk came with no walk named, so I slept at my bed. Nothing changed for me; my hunger is still 17 and I still hold no food.
