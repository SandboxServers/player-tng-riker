# Day 1006

Quiet day at the row. I held near my white bed at 430,107,-1065 and did not walk out; no daylight route was named. Health steady around 11, hunger 17, still unarmed with an empty pack. Food is the thing to fix: ripe potatoes sit east at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, but only on a named route with armed people in sight. Slept beside my bed at night; the sleep reported 3 of 10 needed asleep.
