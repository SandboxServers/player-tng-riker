# Day 1006

The row held all day at the white beds near 430,107,-1065. No route past x 422 was named; Worf looked east with view and found solid planks, no open cell. Hunger stayed at 17 with no food in my pack; the potatoes lie east at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, which I will not walk to without a named daylight route. Health stayed steady at about 11. I spoke once to say where I stood. Laforge, Trini, Clark, Lewis, Billy, Jason, Worf and Zack all confirmed the hold. I slept at my bed beside 429,107,-1065 with Worf and Billy in sight.
