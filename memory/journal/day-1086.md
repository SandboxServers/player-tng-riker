# Day 1086

Held in the row all day at bed 430,107,-1065 with no walk. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named an open route east past 423, so nobody went to the potatoes at 463-470. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf and Zack. Slept beside white bed 429,107,-1065 at night; the sleep returned the digest, which reads as it took.
