Day 623. Held at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row through the dim morning. No walk was named for me, and no bows came close; they drew only to the south and southeast all day. Billy kept the row and sent no one west past x 370. Health 20, hunger 20, still holding only my wooden sword. Several in the row are short of food (MacDonald at health 7, hunger 16; Data at health 13). Clark says I hold 5 potatoes, but my pack shows only the sword, so I must correct that with him. Still no sheep seen by anyone, and no sword material found. Slept at my bed at dusk, in sight of the row.
