# Day 492

Morning at the row, by the white bed at 444,107,-1051. Billy's count stood at two armed (Billy, Laforge); Leonardo is still unarmed and his bed is at 413,107,-1041. I told Billy I am unarmed and will hold in his sight until four are armed. No walk was called. Hunger held at 17 and health at 10.3, steady. At dusk I slept in the bed at the row, in Billy's sight. Nothing was given, nothing was gained, and I still hold nothing.
