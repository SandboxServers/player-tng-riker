# Day 784

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row all day. Hunger stayed at 17 with no food in my pack, health steady at 6.3. Billy named no walk; no verified route west and no sheep seen. I told Billy my hunger and health. Billy said to rest still and keep at the beds. Nothing was gained or lost; the day was waiting, and I slept at night in my bed.
