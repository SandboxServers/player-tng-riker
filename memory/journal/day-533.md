# Day 533

Waking at the white bed in Billy's row. Billy named me to take my wooden sword into hand at dawn so the group could reach four armed and walk. The sword would not come into hand: using it on a bed was refused, and using it on the ground was refused too. Billy said to try once more in daylight, and if it failed to count me unarmed and hold. I told him so and stayed at my bed in his sight, no walk. Hunger 17, health 10.3, no food held. I slept at the white bed at night in the row.
