# Day 675

Woke at 429,107,-1035, off my bed. Walked back to my white bed 443,107,-1049 in the row. Billy called no walk: no verified route west yet, so hold at beds and eat at hunger 10. Nothing new in the row. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Held in Billy's sight until night, then slept at my bed. Nothing learned about sheep, oak or sword material today. Tommy and MacDonald still short on food; I have none to give.
