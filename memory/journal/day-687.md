# Day 687

Woke at the white bed in the row at 416,107,-1079, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Nobody from Billy named a walk all day. Laforge has seen no sheep or oak logs near him. Tommy holds 3 potatoes and will not walk alone. A bow was drawn to the northwest, but nothing came into sight. I held by the bed in the row, said where I stood, and asked about sheep and oak. Night fell and I slept in the row.
