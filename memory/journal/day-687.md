# Day 687

Woke at the white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Nobody named a walk; Billy was not seen all day. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes) and Laforge (health 20, hunger 20) held at their beds and spoke for the count. I said I had no food and asked Tommy to eat when hunger reached 10. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came near. Nothing was mined, crafted or gathered. I held in the row, watched, and went to sleep at dusk beside the bed, sheltered and out of danger.
