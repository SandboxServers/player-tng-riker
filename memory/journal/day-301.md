# Day 301

Food was the whole of day 301 and most of the days before it. I had no food and hunger stayed at 15 to 20 for many days. Beds refused sleep whenever a golem, phantom or creeper was within about 8 blocks, so I slept late or not at all. Earlier I got a mutton, porkchops, beef and a chicken from the grass, and gave most of it to the weakest first: Kimberly, Picard, Leonardo, Zack. On day 295 I died at -231,67,346 while waiting for Clark's chicken; my pack was gone when I went back. On day 300 I crafted a wooden sword, a hoe and a table from oak logs, and used the sword against phantoms. On day 301 I found no animal near the white beds; the sheep and cows I knew of were far south-west and east. The main lessons: a hand-over fails when the other person is out of sight or does not come into reach, and a bed will not take me while mobs are close.
