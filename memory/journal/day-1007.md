# Day 1007

Night in the row, dim and not enclosed. I stayed at my bed 430,107,-1065 beside the white bed, health 11, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. No daylight route was named, so I held. Lewis spoke from his bed and said he was holding too. I told him I would listen for a route at first light. I slept, though sleep only partly took (6 of 10 asleep). Eating is still due at hunger 10 or when named; potatoes are east at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075.
