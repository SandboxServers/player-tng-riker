# Day 447

Night at the bed row, 444,107,-1051, not enclosed, holding nothing, health about 10.3, hunger 17. Nobody came into sight. I said aloud that I hold here until Billy's count. I slept in my bed and the sleep worked. Hunger is still high and I still owe Clark 4 cobblestone, which I do not hold. Tomorrow: wait for Billy's count of four, eat at hunger 6 from Wesley's beef, give nothing unless asked.
