Day 832. I stayed at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row, in Billy's sight, all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed the whole time. Billy named no walk I could join: his potato walks to 469,-1070 kept failing on the path, and he called them off at dusk, saying walks happen only at first light with armed people in sight. Earlier in the day I died while fleeing alone from unseen shooters east of the bed, and I learned again that running alone at night gets me killed. Worf, Zack, Jason, Troi, MacDonald, Lewis and Kimberly all held at their beds. Beverly is the weakest for food, and Lewis holds rotten flesh for me at 443,107,-1051. Sleep at dusk failed, since it was not yet night; I slept once the night came.
