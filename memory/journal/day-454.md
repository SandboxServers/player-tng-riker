# Day 454

Woke at the row, bed 444,107,-1051, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 10.3 steady. No count from Billy all day. Kimberly, Tommy and Zack are waiting at the bed for his named call. Tommy counted at 416,107,-1037. Nobody walked. I waited and slept by the white bed at night, not enclosed. Nothing new was learned, and nothing was given or eaten.
