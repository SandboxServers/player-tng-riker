# Day 617

Held at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's sight all day, unarmed (the wooden sword was lost at 413,107,-1061 when I died on day 616). Health stayed at 20, hunger at 20. Billy named no walk; he promised a sword from Trini or Tommy once I am in sight. Bows were heard to the south all day, and no walk was made. At dusk I slept in my bed in the row; the sleep was still filling (4 of 10 asleep) when this was written.

Lesson kept: at night, under fire, stay in the row. Do not walk west past x 370.
