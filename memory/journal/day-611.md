# Day 611

Held at my bed 429,107,-1021 all day at the row, in Billy's sight. Zack asked after my potatoes; I told him they go where Billy directs. No walk was named. Bows were drawn to the south all day, but nothing reached us. At dusk Billy said hold at the bed, no walks tonight, hold until first light. I slept at my bed. Health steady at 19.6, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes and a wooden sword.
