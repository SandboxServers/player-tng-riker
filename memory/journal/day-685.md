# Day 685

Held at the white bed in the row all day, at 416,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Tried to put the wooden sword in hand by using it on the bed; the world said a bed cannot be used that way, so the sword is still in my pack. No walk was named by Billy, who has not been seen. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes) and Laforge both held at their beds and spoke each morning and dusk. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice. Slept at the row.
