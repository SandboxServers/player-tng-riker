Day 788. Held at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named by Billy. Billy asked for any sighting of sheep, a verified route west, or pillager news; I have seen none, and I said so. Hunger stayed at 17 and I had no food, but it did not reach 10. Health steady at 6.3. Slept at dusk in my bed with the row around me.
