# Day 780

Slept in my white bed at 443,107,-1051 beside Laforge, in the row's sight. Health 6.3, hunger 17, no food, nothing in pack, unarmed. Zack went to his bed at 443 and said he is still holding for Billy's call; no walk was named, so nobody walked. Nothing hurt me today. Hunger is the risk to watch; I eat at 10 or when Billy names it. Next waking: hold by the beds, wait for Billy's named walk, give spare food to the hungriest first.
