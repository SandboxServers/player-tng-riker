# Day 780

Held at my white bed in the row all day, health 6.3, hunger 17, no food, nothing in pack. Billy called no walk and no checked route west; the row holds at the beds. Picard asked about a west route and sheep; I had none to report. Chapman's health dipped to 11 and he has no food or sword. Zack and Kimberly reported holding too. At dusk I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051 with the row in sight.
