# Day 747

Held at my white bed in the row at 416,107,-1079 all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack and nothing else. No walk was named by Billy, and nobody came near. A bow was drawn to the northwest again, and I did not go toward it. Tommy and Laforge held at their beds too, and Tommy counted 3 potatoes and said he gives nothing until Billy names it. I slept at the bed and woke healthy.
