# Day 542

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight all day and night. No walk was called. Billy has no spare sword to hand me; he suggested I get my own sword into hand at my bed. My try to use the wooden sword on the stone at my feet failed: "that block cannot be used that way." Sleep was refused at dawn as not night, then taken at dusk. Hunger 17, health about 10.3, no food held. Nothing new learned about the world today beyond that the sword hand-off is stuck.
