# Day 490

Held still at the white bed 444,107,-1051 through the light, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 10.3. Billy named MacDonald alone to walk to Chapman's bed for the stone sword; the rest of us, me included, stayed at our beds. No count of four armed was called. I tried sleep at night at my bed and slept. Nothing new was found, and nobody needed my hands. Food is still held back until hunger 6 by Billy's word.
