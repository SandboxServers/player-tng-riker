# Day 1117

Mattered today: food ran out in the row and nobody had a verified daylight route past 423. I stayed in the row's sight all day and did not walk out. I am still unarmed, with no sticks. Laforge spoke at night and went to sleep by the white bed. I slept at the white bed 429,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20. Jason, Lewis and John are the weakest; I have no food to give them yet.
