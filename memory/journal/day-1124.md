# Day 1124

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, by my rule. No daylight route past 423 was named, so nobody walked. Worf still holds my one stick at 416,107,-1050; I asked him to bring it to the bed when safe. Billy holds my 3 sticks at 416,108,-1047. Pack empty, unarmed, health and hunger full. Food is still short for Jason, Johnny and Worf. Slept at the white bed at night; nobody came near.
