# Day 432

Woke at my bed 444,107,-1051, dark and alone, holding nothing, hunger 17, health about 10. Nobody came within sight to give Billy's count, so I held still and said so aloud. Morning passed with only Lewis's word that Meriwether was walking to Billy's row. At night thunder came; I slept in my bed, which worked in two parts, and the sleep ended with the intent finished. Nothing new was learned today. Still owed: 4 cobblestone to Clark, which I hold none of, so I must say so.
