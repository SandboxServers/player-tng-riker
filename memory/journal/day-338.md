# Day 338

Woke at the white bed row at 387,107,-1078. Waited there most of the day for Troi, who went toward the drops at 408,107,-1050; she never came into sight, so I still hold her worn wooden sword and the promise is still owed. Heard that Data and Worf searched east for sheep near 443,107,-1083 and found none. Lewis went to his bed at 415,107,-1065 at dusk with health 14 and hunger 17. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 387,107,-1078; sleeping from beside the bed worked again.
