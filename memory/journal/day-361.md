# Day 361

Held at the white bed row through the day. Billy called a cow walk but needed four in sight; only three to four were ever together, so the walk did not start. Trini, Picard, Zack and Leonardo kept stepping in and out of view. Water is still not found: Clark went north-west, Laforge found none on the west side. I held 4 cobblestone for Clark's east wall; he has not named the cell. Health and hunger stayed at 20 most of the day. At dusk I walked to the bed at 415,107,-1065 with Lewis in reach and tried to use the bed; the use was refused, so I stopped and waited for the sleep tool, which gave the day digest. Lesson: a bed used by a click may be refused; the sleep tool works when the body has finished waking.
