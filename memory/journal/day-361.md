# Day 361

Held at the bed row at 416,107,-1065 all day with Lewis in reach. Billy never called the cow hop; he waited for four in sight and then said no walk tonight, to go at waking. I hold no food, health 20, hunger 20, and I gave nothing away. Sleep was refused once from an enderman nearby, then taken at dusk at my bed. Water, food and the east-wall cell are still open. Kimberly and Picard hold food for the lowest.
