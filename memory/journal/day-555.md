# Day 555

Dawn at the row, in Billy's sight, at the white bed 429,107,-1023. Hunger stayed at 17 all day, so I ate nothing and held 7 potatoes. Billy held 21 potatoes for Wesley, who was not at his bed and was later found at his own bed with Worf. Everyone heard was fed, so no food was handed out. Thunder came in the afternoon and we all held at the row. Nothing was lost. The day was holding still, watching Billy's count, and keeping my word to stay put.
