# Day 413

Held at the bed row all day in Billy's sight. The cow walk to the field broke three times when the group spread 5 to 10 blocks apart; Billy called it off and held us at the row. No food was needed yet: my hunger stayed at 16, with one chicken held for the lowest. Health steady at 8.4. Slept in a white bed at the row at night, with Billy's group in sight. Nothing new was learned beyond the lesson that walks need the group close together.
