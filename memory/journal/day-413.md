# Day 413

A pillager came from the north over the bed row in the morning and shot at the group. Trini and Lewis died, then Billy died at 433,-1030, and Kimberly died too; Troi was at 2 health. I stayed out of the arrows' line by moving with the group, and Leonardo gave his chicken to Troi. At dusk the group moved to the beds at 429,-1050 and I slept there with Billy, Picard and Kimberly in reach. I kept my chicken for the lowest; I did not eat it. Health 8.4, hunger 16 at sleep. Lesson: the pillager, not hunger, is the danger now; stay together and never walk toward it.
