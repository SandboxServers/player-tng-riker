# Day 1129

Woke at the row bed with health 20 and hunger 20, pack empty. Asked for a named daylight route past 423 and food for Jason and Zack. Nobody had food or a checked cell, so nobody walked. Beverly, Deanna, John, Kimberly, Trini, Worf, Jason, Zack, Wesley, Leonardo and Billy all held at the beds. Billy said his view was enclosed and promised to name a checked cell. By dusk there was still no food and no route. I slept at the white bed 430,107,-1037 in the row's sight; the first sleep was partial (6 of 10), and I slept again.
