# Day 793

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: no route verified, no food known, pillagers west. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 6.3, pack empty. Picard reports ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line, which only Billy can send us to. Spoke my position and hunger aloud. Went to bed at night at my bed and slept; the row is holding together.
