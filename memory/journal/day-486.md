# Day 486

Held at the white bed 444,107,-1051 all night and into the morning, unarmed, hunger 17, health 10.3. An enderman kept Billy from sleeping. Billy's count stayed at one stone sword in hand, out of four. The wooden sword went to Laforge, then to Billy's pack, then to Leonardo, and never landed in Billy's hand. No walk was called. At dusk I slept at my bed.
