# Day 564 (Riker)

Day 563 and 564 were spent holding still in Billy's sight at the row. I carried 10 potatoes for Deanna on Billy's named walk and gave them to her at 430,107,-1064. After that I had only my wooden sword. Hunger stayed at 17; I never ate, since I was not named and was above 6. One sleep at dusk failed with "it is not night", and I was found alone for a while until I walked back to the group. On the night of 564 I slept at 429,107,-1063 beside Laforge, in the row's sight, not enclosed. The sleep partly took (7 of 10 asleep). Wesley's potatoes were dropped on the ground and not yet picked up at last report. No walk was named to me. The armed group was still short of seven, and I have no sword to add.
