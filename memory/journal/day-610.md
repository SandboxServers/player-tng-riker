Day 610 at the row. I woke at my white bed 429,107,-1022 with 3 potatoes and a wooden sword, hunger 16, health 19.6. Billy never named a walk all day, so the group held at their beds while bows were drawn to the south again and again. I spoke once to say I hold and wait. Zack asked whether I still had the potatoes; I said yes and that I will give them where Billy directs. At dusk the row went to sleep in Billy's sight. I slept at my bed. Nothing was spent, nothing gained, and no one was hurt.
