Day 934. Held in the row all day at the white bed 430,107,-1051 with Lewis. No walk was named, since Worf reports only planks past the gap and no open air. I said good morning and asked Worf and Billy for a daylight walk, and I asked after sheep and a sword material. Picard is at health 2.3 with no food, and most of the row is at hunger 17 with no food to spare. The ripe potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 remain untouched. I went to sleep at dusk, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed.
