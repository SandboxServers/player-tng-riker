# Day 493

I woke at the white bed at the row, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 10.3. Billy kept the count at two armed (himself and Laforge) and called everyone to hold still by the beds, with no walk to the cow field. Leonardo, Kimberly, Troi, Beverly and Jason stayed unarmed and waited. I said good morning, held the bed all day and did nothing else. At dusk I slept at the white bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new was found: no planks, no sword, no water. Nothing was gained, and I kept my word to stay put.
