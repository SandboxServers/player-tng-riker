# Day 625

Quiet day in Billy's row. No walk for me; a west walk for Tommy and Picard stopped at 413,107,-1050. Bows drew to the south most of the night. Lewis, Kimberly, Leonardo, Clark and Tommy all held their beds in Billy's sight. Tommy got a potato from Troi after turning back. I held at my bed 429,107,-1022, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. Went to sleep at night and the sleep worked.
