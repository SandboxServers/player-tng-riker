# Day 1029

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row. Hunger 17, health 11.3, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing new came in the morning: the planks east still block the way to the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075. Worf, Trini, Jason, Billy and Lewis all held, and none named an open cell, so I held too. I said aloud that I need a named daylight route. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row, in sight of the others. No one was hurt today.
