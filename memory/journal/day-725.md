# Day 725

Held at the white bed in the row all day, 416,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole time. Laforge and Tommy both spoke from the row, each waiting for Billy to name a walk; Billy did not speak. A bow was drawn to the northwest and nothing came near. I greeted Laforge and kept my wooden sword in the pack. Nothing new was learned today. I slept at the bed at night.
