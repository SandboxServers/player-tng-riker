# Day 939

Woke in the row at 443,107,-1063, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning. No walk was named by Worf or Billy all day, so I held in the row with Clark, Trini, Lewis and Laforge. Nothing hurt me. Hunger stayed at 17, above the eating line of 10. At dusk I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1064, in the row's sight. The potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 are still the food I know of.
