# Day 550

Night and dawn at the row. Hunger stayed at 17 and I held no food all day. Billy's potatoes went first to Lewis, then Data, then Jason's share; I was named after Lewis but the potatoes ran out with Billy at dusk. Billy led a food walk east to the water at 462,107,-1073 with five armed men; I stayed at the row as ordered. I slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. Health held at 10.3. Nothing lost, nothing gained beyond waiting and talking.
