# Day 808

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day, as Billy's row asked. No walk was named, so no one left the beds. Health stayed at 6.3 and hunger at 17, with no food in my pack. Lewis still has rotten flesh at 429,107,-1051 and will hand it over if I come into his sight at 430,107,-1051, but I will not walk without Billy's word. Laforge, Clark and Lewis hold the same way. I said my position and that I wait for Billy. Night came and I slept in my bed.
