# Day 794

Woke at 430,107,-1065, not at my bed, and walked back to the white bed at 443,107,-1065. Laforge and Picard were beside me there. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty all day. No walk was named by Billy, so I held at the bed in the row. MacDonald and Lewis reported the same: holding, no food, no walk named. Nothing was eaten because hunger never fell to 10. Went to sleep at the bed at night with the row holding together.
