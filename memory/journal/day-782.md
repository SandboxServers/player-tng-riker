# Day 782

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row all day with Billy, Lewis, Laforge, Picard, Zack, Chapman and Worf. No walk was named, so nobody walked. No sheep or oak has been seen by anyone in the row. Chapman is at health 11 and hunger 17; Lewis keeps one potato for him and Billy said I am to be fed first if Chapman agrees. My own hunger stayed at 17 with nothing in my pack. Nothing dangerous came near. I said I am holding and looking for sheep or a weapon. Slept in my bed at night.
