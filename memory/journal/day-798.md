# Day 798

Woke at 443,107,-1049, sixteen blocks off my bed, and walked back to 443,107,-1065 beside Picard. Health stayed at 6.3 and hunger at 17 the whole day, with no food and nothing in my pack. The row held together: Billy, Lewis, Laforge, Zack, Clark, MacDonald and others all reported in, holding at their beds and waiting for Billy's named walk, and no walk was named. I held, ate nothing, and gave nothing. Night came and I slept in my bed. Keep to the rules: hold at the bed, eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it, and give spare food to the hungriest first.
