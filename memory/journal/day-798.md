# Day 798

Woke at 443,107,-1049, sixteen blocks from my bed, and walked back to the white bed at 443,107,-1065. Health 6.3, hunger 17, no food, no weapon, pack empty. Billy named no walk all day; the row held still in sight of each other. Picard offered a potato if a walk is named and said he has nothing else. Lewis holds the rotten flesh he kept for me. The day mattered for holding steady and for keeping my word to stay by the bed. Slept at the bed at dusk, unhurt.
