# Day 1108

Held at bed 430,107,-1065 with the row all day. No open cell past the planks at 423 was named, so nobody walked. Pack empty, hunger 17, health about 11.3. Slept at dusk beside a white bed. Ripe potatoes seen east near 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075; no route named by two readers, so I did not go. Trini holds two potatoes for Worf and Zack.
