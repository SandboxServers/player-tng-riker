# Day 1108

Held at bed 430,107,-1065 with the row all day. No open cell past the planks at 423 was named, so nobody walked. Pack empty, hunger 17, health about 11.3. Went to sleep at dusk beside a white bed. Ripe potatoes are seen east near 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, but no route there has been named by two readers, so I did not go.
