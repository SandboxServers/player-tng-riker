# Day 1108

Held at bed 430,107,-1065 all day. Trini, Worf, Zack, Laforge, Lewis, Clark and Wesley all held in the row, and nobody could name an open cell past the planks at 423, so no one walked. I asked Trini twice to bring her two potatoes to me; she refused, keeping them for Worf and Zack first, which is fair. Hunger stayed at 17, pack empty, health steady at 11.3. Slept at my bed at dusk. Nothing was broken and no one was hurt.
