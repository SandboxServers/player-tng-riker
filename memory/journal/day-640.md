# Day 640

Held all day at my white bed in Billy's row, at 444,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. No walk was named, so nobody moved. Billy did not call a walk. The row kept its word and held together: MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are still short, and Zack keeps two potatoes for anyone who drops to hunger 10. I have no food to give. Nothing new was learned about sheep or a sword material. Slept in place at dusk, in sight of Billy.
