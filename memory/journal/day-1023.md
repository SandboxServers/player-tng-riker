# Day 1023

Quiet day at the row. I woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 at night, dim light, health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named an open route east, so I stayed in the row. Clark said good night and is holding at his bed. I said goodnight to the row and slept beside my bed; the sleep worked with 7 of 10 needed asleep. Still hungry at 17, with potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 when a safe route is named. Nothing new was learned today.
