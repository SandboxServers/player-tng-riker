# Day 1023

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named an open route east to the potatoes all day; Billy, Jason and Zack each saw only solid planks past 423. I said I hold and have no food to give. Nothing was walked, nothing broken. At dusk the row went to its beds and I slept beside my white bed, in sight of the row. Still hungry, still holding; the potatoes at 463-470 remain the only known food.
