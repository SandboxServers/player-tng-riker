# Day 1068

Held in the white bed row all day and night. No one could name an open daylight cell east past x 423, so no one walked. Hunger stayed at 17 and the pack stayed empty; I did not eat, since I was waiting for hunger 10 or a named turn. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack; I am not on her list yet. Slept beside bed 429,107,-1065 at dusk. Health 11.3, steady.
