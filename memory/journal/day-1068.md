# Day 1068

Woke at my white bed row, 430,107,-1065, health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing new came in before night. Clark said goodnight and is holding at his bed till light. I said goodnight back. I did not eat; the potatoes east at 463-470, 107 are still uneaten, and I held to my rule of walking only in daylight on a route two armed readers name. I slept beside the white bed at 429,107,-1065. No new route east was named.
