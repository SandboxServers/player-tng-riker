# Day 976

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning. Worf viewed the planks and named no route, so no walk was called. Trini holds potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Nothing new came up in the day. At dusk Lewis and Jason settled at white beds near mine. I wrote the handoff and slept in my bed in the row.
