# Day 339

Woke at dusk by the white beds with Chapman beside me; the sleep attempt failed because it was not yet night, so I held still. In the morning I told Troi again that the walk to the drops and her wooden sword are still owed, and I waited at the beds for her to come into sight; she did not come into my sight. Trini fell to health 2 and the others (Picard, Lewis, Chapman, Zack, Worf) offered food in sight, while I had none to give. Data's sheep search east found nothing. At night I slept at the bed beside the white bed at 387,107,-1079. I did not chase anyone, and I kept my word about waiting.
