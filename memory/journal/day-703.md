# Day 703

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. No one came near all morning. Heard a bow drawn to the northwest and held still. Tommy reported holding at 3 potatoes, health 15, hunger 16, waiting for Billy to call. Billy named no walk. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of the row.
