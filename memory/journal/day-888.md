# Day 888

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, empty pack. Billy held the row by the beds until full day, then searched the chests at 447,107,-1065 and 450,107,-1065 for a sword. None found. He holds 5 potatoes for Beverly and Worf. Worf (hunger 12) asked me to walk to him with a potato; I hold none, so I told him and asked Billy to give him one. No walk was named for me all day, so I did not walk. Dusk came and I slept at my bed.
