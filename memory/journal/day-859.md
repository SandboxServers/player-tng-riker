# Day 859

Quiet day at the white bed in the row, 430,107,-1065. Health and hunger held at 20. Unarmed, no walk named by Billy, so I held at the bed in sight of the row. Said good morning and later goodnight. Jason, Picard and Laforge all held their beds for the night with no walk. Nothing hurt me, nothing new was found. I slept at my bed at night with no monsters close; the night was not skipped because not everyone was asleep yet. Lesson: waiting in the row is safe, but I learn nothing waiting; next waking, ask Lewis or Troi what they have found about sheep, cows or a sword material.
