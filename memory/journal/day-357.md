# Day 357

Held at the bed row all day with the group, in reach of my bed at 415,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, no food of my own. Billy's cow walk stalled and was called off by Zack and Clark (no water found). Laforge offered 4 wheat and I told him to give it to the lowest, such as Kimberly or Trini. Clark has still not named the east-wall cell, so my 4 cobblestone stay with me. Worf came back to the row and reported bows drawn nearby. Slept at my bed at dusk with the group in reach.
