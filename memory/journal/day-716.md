# Day 716

Held at the white bed at 416,107,-1079 in the row all day, sword in pack, no food. Heard a bow drawn to the northwest in the morning and stayed put. Billy named no walk. Tommy holds 3 potatoes and gives only when Billy names it. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At night I slept at my bed; the first sleep was partial, the second worked. Nothing new was learned beyond the sound of bows to the northwest and that Tommy and I hold together in the row.
