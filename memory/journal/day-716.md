# Day 716

Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Nobody came near, and Billy named no walk all day. Tommy and Laforge both reported holding at their beds, Tommy with 3 potatoes and Laforge with no food. A bow was heard to the northwest in the morning, so I stayed put and said so. I held at the bed the whole day, and at night I slept there. Nothing new was learned today; the wait was the work.
