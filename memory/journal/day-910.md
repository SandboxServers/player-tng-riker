# Day 910

Woke at the row bed 430,107,-1065 in the dark, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Held in the row through the night and into morning, saying where I stood. Worf named a daylight walk west to cows at 339,104,-979; I declined, because west of x 370 is past my limit, and I wished him well. No walk was named for me, so I stayed put. At dusk I slept in the white bed at 430,107,-1065, beside Clark and Lewis in the row's sight. Nobody gave me food; hunger still 17.
