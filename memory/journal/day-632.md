# Day 632
Held in Billy's row at my bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, no walk named. Billy still has no verified west route, so nobody walked. MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) and Tommy (hunger 16) are short; Clark holds the potatoes and will bring them at hunger 10. I have no food to give. I said I hold, and went to bed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight.
