# Day 469

Held at the white bed at 444,107,-1051 through the night and the next day, unarmed, hunger 17, health 10.3, holding nothing. Billy called no walk: he counted only Laforge and Beverly in sight, and the stone sword passed to Laforge after a slot was freed with wheat seeds. Billy and Kimberly also worked out a bed meeting at the row. I was never in his sight, so I was not offered anything and did not walk. I slept at the bed at night with the group near. No one has offered me food yet; I will ask Laforge for a potato at hunger 6.
