# Day 340

Woke at dawn at the white beds at 388,107,-1079. Held still in sight of the group. Gave Troi her worn wooden sword in her sight, which keeps my word to her. She agreed to walk to the drops at 408,107,-1050 tomorrow in daylight, in each other's sight. Still owed. Everyone else was fed or sharing; Trini, MacDonald and Clark were low. I had no food and did not chase anyone. Slept at dusk beside the bed at 389,107,-1078.
