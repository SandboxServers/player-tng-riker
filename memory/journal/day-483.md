# Day 483

Held at the white bed at the row, 444,107,-1051, all day. No walk was called by Billy. Kimberly, Beverly and Macdonald are holding too, all unarmed. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 10.3, steady. Nothing was taken or gained. I said good morning and told the group I was holding. Slept at the bed at dusk.
