# Day 684

Quiet day by the white beds at 415,107,-1079. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Nobody named a walk; Billy still unseen. Laforge and Tommy spoke at morning: both holding in the row for Billy's call. Tommy is at health 15, hunger 16 and holds 3 potatoes. I held still, did not go west of x 370, and slept beside the bed at dusk.
