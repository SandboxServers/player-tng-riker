# Day 719

Held at my bed in the row all day, no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Tommy holds 3 potatoes and gives only when Billy names it; he will eat when his hunger drops below 15. Laforge holds at his bed with no food. A bow was drawn to the northwest at night. I said my hold aloud and promised Tommy food first when I find some. Slept at my bed.
