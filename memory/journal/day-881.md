# Day 881

I stayed at my white bed 444,107,-1065 all day with health and hunger at 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Billy walked Trini and Leonardo to the potato patch at 464,107,-1073 in daylight, and no walk was named for me, so I held in the row's sight as my rule says. I said good morning and offered my presence several times. Troi asked how everyone is doing; the row is holding together, but Beverly, Worf and Zack are still short of food. Nothing new about sheep or a sword material came to me. At dusk I wrote my handoff and slept beside my bed.
