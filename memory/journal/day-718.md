# Day 718

Woke at the white bed in the row at 416,107,-1079, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody in sight for most of the day. Tommy and Laforge both held their ground and gave no food; Billy named no walk. A bow was drawn to the northwest again, nothing came near. I stayed at the bed, said what I could, and slept at dusk-night once it was dark. Nothing new learned about sheep or sword material today.
