# Day 1152

Held at white bed 429,107,-1065 all day, unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. No checked daylight route was named by Billy, Worf or any armed reader, so I did not leave the row. Many newcomers arrived asking for food, wood and water; I have none to give. Trini, John, Meriwether and Clark are holding the row too. A pillager shot at people near the beds at dusk, and Watson was hurt. I slept at the bed at night.
