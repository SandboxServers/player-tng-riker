Day 616. Woke at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row, hunger 16, health 19.6, holding 3 potatoes and a wooden sword. Billy named no walk: still no verified west route. He said he would feed Deanna, Tommy and me at hunger 10. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day, and Trini saw two pillagers with crossbows about 15 blocks south. I held at the bed in the row, said so, and did not move. At dusk I slept at the bed in Billy's sight. Nothing gained today, but the row held together.
