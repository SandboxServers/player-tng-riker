# Day 1101

Held in the row all night at bed 430,107,-1065. At light I walked to 424,107,-1053 to see plank 420,108,-1051 and Worf at 419,108,-1047. I could see neither, and nobody else has named the plank from their own sight, so I broke nothing. Trini holds two potatoes for Worf but cannot see him through the wall; Worf cannot see her either. Hunger still 17, pack empty, no food given. At dusk I returned to bed and slept. Nothing has been broken and nobody has been fed yet.
