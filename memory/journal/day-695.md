# Day 695

Quiet day in the row. Woke at the white bed with health 20 and hunger 20, the wooden sword in my pack and no food. Billy named no walk, so I held by the bed. I opened the chest three blocks east; it gave no food I could see. Laforge and Tommy held at their beds too. Tommy is still at health 15 and hunger 16, with three potatoes he has promised to MacDonald. A bow was drawn to the northwest and nothing came near. I went back to the bed at dusk and slept.
