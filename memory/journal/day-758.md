Day 758. Held at my white bed in the row, 416,107,-1079, all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Laforge and Tommy held near at their beds; Tommy has 3 potatoes and is at health 15, hunger 16. No walk was named by Billy. A bow was drawn to the northwest at dawn; nothing came near. Nothing new learned; the day was holding and waiting, and I slept at dusk in bed.
