# Day 758

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, in the row with Tommy and Laforge. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20, still only the wooden sword. Tommy reported 3 potatoes and hunger 16, and I said so aloud. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came near. I slept at dusk in my bed, nothing changed.
