# Day 1063
The row held all day at the white beds. No open cell past 423 was named by any armed reader, so nobody walked east or to the potatoes. Billy looked east at light and saw only planks. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo. I stayed at bed 430,107,-1065, unarmed, hunger 17, health 11.3, pack empty. Slept at dusk; the day's events came back as speech only. Nothing new learned about the world beyond what the row already knows.
