# Day 726

Held at my white bed in the row at 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Nothing came near; only a bow drawn to the northwest was heard. Billy named no walk, so I waited, as my goals say. Tommy and Laforge still hold at their row beds with no word from Billy. Tommy has 3 potatoes and is short on food; I have none to give. Slept at the bed at night. Nothing new learned today. Curious still: where sheep graze, where oak logs and a sword material might come from. Tell Billy, Tommy and Laforge when the walk is named.
