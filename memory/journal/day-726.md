# Day 726

Held at my white bed in the row at 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Billy named no walk. Tommy (3 potatoes, health 15, hunger 16) and Laforge (no food) held at their row beds too. A bow was drawn to the northwest on the dawn of 725; nothing came near.
