# Day 472

Woke at the row by the white bed. Billy kept the list empty: no count of four, no walk. Two armed (Laforge, Trini). Held at the row in Billy's sight all day, unarmed, hunger 17, no food. Said I hold for the count. At dusk I tried to use the bed beside me; the world said that block is a bed and cannot be used that way, so I slept by the group instead. Nothing was lost. Owe Clark 4 cobblestone; hold none. Learned: Billy's count only counts those in his view with a sword in hand.
