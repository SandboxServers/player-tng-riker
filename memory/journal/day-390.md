# Day 390

Held in Billy's row all day at 416,107,-1037. Billy counted the group several times but never called the cow walk, so I did not walk. No food came to me; hunger 16, health 8.4 with no regeneration. Slept beside the bed at 415,107,-1037 at dusk, not on it. Food known: ripe wheat and potatoes near 462-463,107,-1073 to -1087 by the water, but that is a walk alone, which I will not do.
