# Day 953

Woke in the row at night beside my bed at 443,107,-1065, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Held by the bed for a named daylight walk; nobody from Worf or Billy named one. Clark and Lewis are also holding at their beds, Lewis at health 20 with no food. Said goodnight to the row and slept beside the white bed. Nothing new found today: no sheep, no sword material. Potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 still the known food.
