# Day 779

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1065 in the row all day, beside Billy, Lewis, Laforge, Zack, Chapman and Picard. No walk was named, and nobody went west past x 370. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3 with no food in my pack; Lewis kept his potato for Chapman, and Billy has no food or route to give. Worf is at 14 and Billy asked for anyone at 10 or below to step into sight. I slept at night in my bed. Still unarmed, still no sheep or verified route seen.
