# Day 779

Quiet day in the row. Woke at my white bed at 443,107,-1051 with health 6.3 and hunger 17, no food, unarmed. Billy named no walk all day and told me to rest at my bed. Laforge, Lewis, Picard, Chapman, Bcrusher all held at their beds in Billy's sight. I told the row where I was. Nothing was learned about food, weapons or sheep. At dusk I slept in my bed. Nothing new was found.
