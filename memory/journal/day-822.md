# Day 822

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 at dusk. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named a walk, so I held in the row. Night came; Clark, Lewis, Beverly, Zack, Jason, Worf, Laforge, Picard and I all said we were holding for Billy's word at first light. Zack's bed at 415,107,-1050 says the way is blocked. Worf holds one potato for whoever is weakest in his sight. I said goodnight in the row and slept in my bed.
