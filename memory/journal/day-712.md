# Day 712

Held at my white bed in the row all day, in sight of Laforge and Tommy. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No walk was named by Billy. At night I told the row I was going to rest and went to sleep at the bed. The sleep returned at once with no new day, so the day is still 712 in the record. Nothing new was learned today; the group keeps holding still for Billy's word.
