# Day 728

Held at my white bed in the row, 416,107,-1079, all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Billy named no walk. Tommy beside me with 3 potatoes, hunger 16, health 15. Laforge holding at his bed, no food. A bow was drawn to the northwest again at midday; nothing came near. Laforge has seen no sheep yet. Nothing new to act on. I slept at the bed at night.
