Day 728. Woke at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Nobody came near. A bow was drawn to the northwest again at dawn and nothing came. Billy named no walk. Laforge holds at his row bed with no food. Tommy holds too, still with his potatoes, and said he will not sleep alone; I told him I am at my bed two beds from him and will sleep here. Slept at my bed at night. Nothing new learned; the holding continues.
