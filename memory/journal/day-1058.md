# Day 1058

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in dim night, health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty. Morning came and the whole row held: nobody could name an open cell past the planks at 425. I viewed west from my bed and saw only planks, so the route to the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 is still shut. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf, then Zack. Nobody ate. I slept at dusk at my white bed, with the handoff written first.
