# Day 667

Held in the row all day. Billy gathered the armed at the middle chest 447,107,-1037 and named an escort walk to the oak at 414,104,-1126, with Zack, Picard and me. He stopped it when we were not in his sight, and held the walk until two armed were in hand. Only Picard had his sword in hand. I tried to ready my wooden sword by using it on the chest; that failed because the chest is a container. Zack has the same trouble. No walk was taken and no one was hurt. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. I went to bed at the white bed 443,107,-1036 in Billy's sight.
