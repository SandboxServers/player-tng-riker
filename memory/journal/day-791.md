# Day 791

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row all day. No food, pack empty, unarmed. Health 6.3, hunger 17. No walk was named by Billy. Picard, Laforge, Lewis and MacDonald all report the same: no food, no sheep, no verified route, no pillager news. Nothing changed. I asked Picard for food or a spare sword; no answer yet. Slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of the row.
