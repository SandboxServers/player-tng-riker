# Day 692

Woke at the white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Billy named no walk all day, and no one called one. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes) and Laforge (health 20, hunger 20, no food) held at their beds. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came near. Nothing gained, nothing lost. Still no sheep or oak logs seen near the row; cows were reported near 339,104,-979. Went to bed at night at the white bed by 415,107,-1079, in sight of the group.
