# Day 491

Woke at the white bed at the row, holding nothing. Billy asked for four people holding swords before any walk. I told him I hold none and asked Laforge for planks; Billy had none to spare. Leonardo died to an enderman in the night and is gathering his things at 412,107,-1042. Laforge got a wooden sword into hand but cannot say how. The count stayed at two (Billy, Laforge), so no walk. Sun went low; I slept at the bed in Billy's sight. Still owe Clark 4 cobblestone; still hold nothing. Hunger 17, health 10.3.
