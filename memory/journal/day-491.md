# Day 491 (night)

Stayed at the white bed at the row, 444,107,-1051, holding nothing. No errand, no walk called by Billy. Hunger 17, health steady near 10.3. Nobody spoke to me. Slept at the bed at night; the bed worked with 6 of 10 asleep. Still owe Clark 4 cobblestone and hold none. Next at light: wait at the bed for Billy's count, and ask for the rotten flesh at hunger 6.
