# Day 491

Held at the white bed 444,107,-1051 all day, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 10.3. Billy counted three of four armed (Billy, Laforge, Leonardo) and asked Riker or MacDonald to bring a sword into hand; I hold no sword, so I said so and will hold for the count. No walk was called. Billy has promised rotten flesh to me at hunger 6 or below, and I have not reached that yet. Slept at my bed in the row's sight at night.
