# Day 1059

Held in the row all day at my white bed 430,107,-1065. Nobody named an open cell past the planks at 425, so no walk toward the potatoes. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Said my morning line aloud. Slept at dusk beside the bed with the row in sight. Nothing new came in. Next light: view west again, and eat at hunger 10 or when named.
