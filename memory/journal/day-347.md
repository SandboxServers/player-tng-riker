# Day 347

Quiet day at the white bed row. I woke at full health and hunger, offered my oak location near 459,110,-1103 for wood, and found the group still short of food. Clark and MacDonald were low; Worf gave them mutton. Nobody has named the cow walk or Clark's east-wall cell yet, so I placed nothing. Troi did not come into sight for the walk owed to her. At dusk I slept at the bed row and the day ended with no harm done.
