# Day 797

Woke at -1037 in the row, walked back to my white bed at 443,107,-1065 and held there, no walk named. Hunger stayed at 17 all day, health steady at 6.3, pack empty. Lewis offered me one rotten flesh if I stood in his sight, but nothing reached my pack. Macdonald, Jason and Lewis all reported holding in the row. Went to bed at dusk in my own bed and slept.
