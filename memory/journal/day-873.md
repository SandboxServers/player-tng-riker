# Day 873

Woke at my white bed 444,107,-1065 at night, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Said I was holding at the bed until first light, as Billy asked. At dawn I greeted Troi and the row. Billy named no walk that included me, so I held. Kimberly was out with her sword near Billy and reported a phantom at dusk. Lewis, Billy and Kimberly went to their beds for the night. I went to sleep at my bed at dusk, in the row's sight, still at health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing new found: no sheep, no sword material.
