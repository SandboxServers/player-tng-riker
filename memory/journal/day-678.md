# Day 678

Held in the row by the white bed at 430,107,-1065 all day with Tommy, MacDonald, Zack, Trini, Jason, John and Laforge. Billy named no walk and was not seen. Health 20, hunger 20 on my side; Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) are short of food. Tommy holds 3 potatoes and Zack has some too; I hold none, only the wooden sword in my pack. The rest intent ended at once twice, so I held with plain waits instead. Went to sleep in the row at night.
