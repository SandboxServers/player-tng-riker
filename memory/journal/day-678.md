# Day 678

Held in the row all day and into night beside my white bed at 430,107,-1065. Billy was not seen and named no walk, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword still in my pack, not in hand. The row is steady: Tommy, MacDonald, Zack, Trini, Jason, John, Picard, Laforge, Worf all holding for Billy's call. MacDonald and Tommy are short on food; Zack holds 3 potatoes for them if named. I spoke my holding line, then slept in the bed at dusk. Sleep returned with nothing new.
