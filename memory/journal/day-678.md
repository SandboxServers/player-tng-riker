# Day 678

Held in the row all day at 430,107,-1065 beside my white bed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy was not seen and named no walk. Tommy, MacDonald, Zack, Trini, Jason, John, Picard and Laforge all held for his call. MacDonald (health 11) and Tommy (health 15) are short on food; Zack holds 3 potatoes. I hold no food and my wooden sword is still in my pack, not my hand. The rest intent ends at once, so waiting is the real work here. Slept at dusk in the row.
