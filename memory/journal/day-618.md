# Day 618

Billy named one walk: east-south to Trini at 444,107,-1023 for a spare sword. Trini's swords had gone to Lewis by mistake; Lewis passed one to me there. I walked back to my bed at 429,107,-1022 with the wooden sword, health 20, hunger 20. Sleeping at night in Billy's sight; sleep only partly took (5 of 10 asleep). Pillagers still shoot from the south all night. Held to the row's named calls throughout.

Next: hold at the row, eat at hunger 6 or when Billy names it. Curious about a better sword material.
