# Day 996

Held at my white bed in the row all day, 430,107,-1065. Health about 11, hunger 17, unarmed, no food. Nobody walked: Worf and Billy would not send anyone out until the route had exact open cells, and the north-west gap showed only our own people. Everyone in the row (Zack, Jason, Trini, Lewis, Clark, Worf, Billy) said the same: hold, and walk only on a named daylight route with an armed leader in sight. I said so aloud and asked Worf and Billy to name the route. Slept at night beside the bed. Nothing was gained or lost today, and the hunger problem is still open; the potatoes at 463-470 are east, past what we have named safe.
