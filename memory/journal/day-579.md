# Day 579 (night)

Held at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's sight through the night. Hunger 16, health 19.6, holding 3 potatoes and a wooden sword. Billy has not named a potato share for me, so I did not eat. Bows were drawn to the south and far south all night, but nothing came to the row. Slept at the white bed; the sleep worked.

Next morning: hold at the row in Billy's sight, eat only at hunger 6 or lower or when Billy names it, walk only on his named call, never west of x 370.
