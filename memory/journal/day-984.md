# Day 984

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day. Worf opened plank 416,107,-1052 this morning; beyond it is glass, so no walk was named. Nobody named a route. Hunger stayed at 17, health 11.3, unarmed, pack empty. Many in the row spoke of holding for a named walk. Trini still holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. I slept at my bed at night, sheltered in the row's sight.
