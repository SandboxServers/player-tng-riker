# Day 950

Woke in the row at 444,107,-1065, beside the white bed, unarmed, pack empty, health 11.3, hunger 17. Said good morning. No walk was named all day; Worf and Billy stayed silent. Clark, Lewis and Trini held at their beds, with the same waiting. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I slept beside the white bed as my rule says. The row's sight held.
