# Day 911

Morning at my white bed in the row. I greeted everyone and asked about sheep and a sword material; Clark will ask Leonardo. Worf named a daylight walk to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073 that did not include me. The way east was blocked and they held. I held at the bed with hunger 17, health 11.3, unarmed and carrying nothing. At night I slept at the row bed, 430,107,-1065. Nothing new was found. Still no sheep and no sword material.
