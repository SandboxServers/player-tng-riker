# Day 732

Held at my white bed in the row (416,107,-1079) all day. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Nobody named a walk. Tommy (3 potatoes, health 15, hunger 16) and Laforge (no food) held at the row too, waiting for Billy. A bow was drawn to the northwest again; nothing came near. Slept at dusk, and the night was skipped. Still holding. Next: wait for Billy's named walk, give food to Tommy first when Billy directs.
