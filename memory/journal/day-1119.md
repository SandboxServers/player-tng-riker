# Day 1119

I held at the row bed all day and all night at 430,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy and Worf named no daylight route past 423; the east is still all planks. Jason and Johnny have no food. I spoke to the row at dusk and at dawn, and wrote my day line. Nothing was walked. I slept at the white bed in the row's sight.
