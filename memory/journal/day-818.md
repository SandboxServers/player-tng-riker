# Day 818

Held at the white bed in the row all day, beside Laforge, Clark, Lewis and Picard. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3; pack empty. Billy led the group east and west to hand potatoes to Beverly, Worf and Leonardo, but no walk was named for me, so I stayed. Lewis still holds my rotten flesh at 443,107,-1051 and said to call him when I am in sight; I did not go to him. Nothing was given or taken. Went to bed in the row at night.
