# Day 487

Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night. I held at the white bed 444,107,-1051 through the day, unarmed with an empty pack, hunger 17, health steady at 10.3. Billy kept the row on no walk: one armed in hand, the stone sword, and he would not call a walk until four were armed. Leonardo was to get two rotten flesh via Laforge. At night I reached the bed again, Beverly and others sleeping in Billy's sight, and I stayed put. Nothing was gained today except holding still, and that was the right call.
