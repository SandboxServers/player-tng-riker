# Day 227

I slept in the yellow bed by the camp table and I am now sleeping through to day 228.

Evening: I walked about 77 blocks south to Kimberly at -277, 67, 297 and handed her the apple. She is eating it now. I walked back toward the yellow beds at night, and the walk stopped once on the way (the way was blocked, then the second try reached the beds). Billy, Trini, Jason and Tommy are sleeping in beds near camp with no food. Iron golems wander north of camp and a bow was drawn somewhere east, so I kept to the beds.
