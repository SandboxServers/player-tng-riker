Day 1112 ended at night in the row, beside the white bed at 430,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Nothing new was found or learned today; I said I was holding in the row and went to sleep beside the bed. The row held and no one spoke to me. I will keep to the rules: no walking at night, no walking west past x 370, and walk only on a named daylight route with an armed reader in sight.
