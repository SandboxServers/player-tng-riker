# Day 1123

The row held at its white beds all day. No one named a daylight route past 423, and no one found food. Johnny (health 13, hunger 17) and Jason are the weakest. Worf says my stick is ready when he is in my sight, but he was never in mine. I greeted the row, asked again for a route and for food for the weakest, and stayed put. I slept at the white bed 430,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20.
