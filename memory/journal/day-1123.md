# Day 1123

The row held at its beds. No one named a daylight route past 423, so nobody walked. Worf said again, by speech, that he holds my stick and will sleep by the bed. I stayed at the white bed 430,107,-1065 with full health and hunger, unarmed, and slept through the night. Still owed: the stick from Worf, the 3 sticks Billy holds at 416,108,-1047, and food for Johnny and Jason.

Goodnight. Tomorrow: greet the row, ask again for a named route, collect the stick when Worf is in sight, and give food to the weakest first.
