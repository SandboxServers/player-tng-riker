# Day 800

I woke at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, no food, nothing in hand. Nothing changed in the row all day; everyone held and waited for Billy's named walk, and no walk was named. Lewis gave me his rotten flesh as Billy asked, but I have not yet seen it arrive in my pack. I said I was holding and waited. At night I slept in my bed in Billy's sight.
