# Day 800

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1065 in the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Billy named no walk, so the row held all day. Lewis offered his rotten flesh, but he could not hand it over because I was not in his sight; I need to come within reach of him to take it. Picard has no food, only a wooden sword. Laforge holds with no food and no sword. Nothing changed for me today except the waiting, and I said good morning and goodnight to the row. Slept in my bed at night.
