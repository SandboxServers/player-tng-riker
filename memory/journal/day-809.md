# Day 809

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row. Hunger 17, health 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy all day, and no food reached me. Said good morning and then held at the bed, waiting for Billy's word. Zack still holds two potatoes for Worf. Clark, Lewis, Laforge and Zack all reported holding too. Nothing new was found. I went to sleep in my bed at dusk, not in danger.
