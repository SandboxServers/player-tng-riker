# Day 488

Held at the white bed in the row all day, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 10.3. Billy called no walk and no count; he said he would count once four are armed in hand, and only Billy holds a stone sword. Billy offered two rotten flesh once I reach hunger 6 or below. Beverly and Troi held with us; MacDonald spoke from the row. Nothing was gained or lost. I slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
