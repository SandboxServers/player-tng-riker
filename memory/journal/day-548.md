Day 548. Held at the row in Billy's sight all day. No food found; hunger stayed at 17 and health at 10.3. Billy named a wheat-and-potato walk east-southeast to 462,107,-1073, then put it on hold because only Jason came; seven armed is still not reached. Lewis and I get food first when any turns up. Cows reported at 339,104,-979 (west, past pillagers) and 480,107,-1137 (night danger). Nothing learned new beyond where food is said to be.
