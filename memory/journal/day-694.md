# Day 694

Held still in the row all day beside the white bed at 416,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Billy named no walk. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes) and Laforge (health 20, no food) held at their beds and spoke at dawn and dusk. A bow was drawn to the northwest once; nothing came near. Sleep tool reported 9 of 10 asleep at the start of the day, and I slept again at night. Nothing new was learned about sheep or oak logs. Still waiting on Billy.
