# Day 453

Night at the row, bed 444,107,-1051. Nobody walked. Billy has not called a walk. Zack reported holding at the row, MacDonald alone at 430,107,-1051 and both waiting on Billy's count. I said goodnight in the open and slept in my bed, not enclosed, holding nothing, hunger 17, health about 10.3. Owe Clark 4 cobblestone; hold none. Waiting for Billy's count is most of the day here.
