# Day 1080

Night in the row at 430,107,-1065. Hunger 17, health 11.3, pack empty, unarmed. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf and Zack. Lewis and Clark are holding in sight. Tried to use the white bed by a plain use; it was refused ("that block is a bed"), so I used the sleep verb instead. Nothing new was found on the route east. The potatoes at 463-470 are too far to reach safely at night. Tomorrow at light: eat or get food from Trini, give Worf food first, and read the route with armed friends.
