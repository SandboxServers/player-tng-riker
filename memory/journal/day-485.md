Day 485. I woke at the white bed 444,107,-1051, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 10.3. Billy kept the count at the row and called no walk. He asked Laforge for a stone sword and Picard for a wooden one: the stone sword landed in Billy's hand, making him one of four armed, but Picard's wooden sword landed in Billy's pack instead, so the count stayed at one. A bow was drawn to the south, and Kimberly stayed by her bed. I held at my bed all day and did not walk. At dusk I slept at the bed with the sleep call, and it worked.
