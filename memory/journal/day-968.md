# Day 968

I woke in the row and held at my white bed at 444,107,-1065 for a walk named by Worf or Billy. None was named through two days. Lewis, Clark and Trini held too. Pillagers came from the south and shot Lewis at his bed; I stayed at my bed, unarmed, and did not go south. Hunger stayed at 17 and I had no food. I slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing was learned new except that the row is held by pillagers from the south.
