# Day 968

Woke in the row at night, at 444,107,-1065, beside the white bed. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. No one near, no errands, no danger. I said good morning to the row, then slept by the bed. Nothing new happened. Next waking: hold for a named daylight walk with armed people, and eat at hunger 10 or when named. Potatoes are at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075; Picard and Leonardo first.
