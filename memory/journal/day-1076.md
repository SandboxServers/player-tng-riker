# Day 1076

The row held all day beside the white beds at 429-443, 107, -1065 to -1050. No one named a daylight walk with exact cells, so no one went east. I stayed at bed 430,107,-1065 with no food and no weapon, hunger 17 and health 11.3 steady. Worf was at hunger 5 with no food; Trini held potatoes for him at the bed. I did not eat, because the potatoes at 463-470 lie outside the row and it was not named safe. Lewis said goodnight from his bed. I slept beside my white bed with the sleep verb and it worked.
