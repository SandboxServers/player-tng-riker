# Day 785

Woke at my white bed in the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, nothing in the pack. Billy named no walk all day, so nobody walked. Lewis is keeping a potato for Chapman, to be given at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Worf asked after sheep and a sword, and nobody has seen any. I held by my bed in Billy's sight, said so aloud, and slept at night in my bed without incident.
