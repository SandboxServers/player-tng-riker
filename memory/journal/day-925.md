# Day 925

Woke in my white bed at 430,107,-1065 in the row. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning to Zack. Worf, Lewis, Laforge, Jason and Zack all held in the row, waiting for Worf to name a daylight route. Worf still had no open cell to name, only plank walls. No walk named, so I did not walk. Dusk came with no change; I wrote the handoff and slept in my bed in the row's sight. No sheep seen, no sword material found.
