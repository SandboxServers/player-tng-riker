# Day 825

Nothing died today and no walk was named for me. I held at my white bed in the row, hunger 17 and health 6.3 all day, pack empty and unarmed. Billy led the row, went to the beds with potatoes and named Lewis as his escort; he gave the potatoes out in sight and turned to Beverly and Bcrusher first, who are the weakest. Wesley asked where cows graze, away from the west, and nobody knew. A phantom struck Billy in the night and Lewis stood guard, though no one saw it again by morning. I took no food, gave none, and kept to the row. Health did not move.
