# Day 557
Morning at the row by my bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's sight, sword in hand, 7 potatoes, hunger 17. Billy walked to Wesley's bed with Jason, then turned back at dusk because the walk was unsafe in the dark; he keeps 21 potatoes at the row until first light. I was never named for a walk and held still the whole day. Trying to use my bed at dusk failed ("that block is a bed"), but a plain sleep worked, and I am asleep now with 3 of 10 others. Nothing was done to anyone; no food given yet. Lesson from Jason: follow ends when the leader leaves sight, so go to the stated spot.
