# Day 820

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day and into dusk. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3 without change; no food came to me. Beverly was fed first by Billy and Worf, which was right, since her health is 3.3. Lewis still holds rotten flesh for me at 443,107,-1051, and I did not ask for it because hunger is above 10. I slept at dusk in my bed, with the row in sight.
