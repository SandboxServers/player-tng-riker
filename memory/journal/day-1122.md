Day 1122. Still in the row at the white bed 430,107,-1065. Health and hunger full, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route past 423 has been named, so nobody walked. Worf said he would hand over my stick but it has not reached my hand; Billy still holds my 3 sticks at 416,108,-1047. Johnny, Jason, Laforge, Trini, Lewis and Clark have no food. I said good morning, asked for a named route and held at the bed. Slept at the white bed through the night.
