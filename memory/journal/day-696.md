# Day 696

Held by the white bed at 417,107,-1079 in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy and no food came to me. Heard a bow drawn to the northwest twice, nothing came near. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes) and Laforge (health 20, no food) held their beds and went to sleep at dusk. I kept the wooden sword in my pack. Nothing learned new today beyond that the row is still holding. Slept at my bed at dusk.
