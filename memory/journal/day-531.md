Day 531. No food came to me all day; hunger held at 17 and health steady at 10.3. Billy called a count at dawn and I answered it, holding at the white bed in his row with no walk. Billy gathered the rotten flesh that Picard and MacDonald brought, and he will eat it when his hunger reaches 6. I said my state aloud once, and I said my hunger was 20 when it was 17, which I should correct. I slept at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's sight at night.
