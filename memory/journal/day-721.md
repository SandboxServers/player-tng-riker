# Day 721

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row all day, with no walk named by Billy. Tommy (3 potatoes, health 15, hunger 16) and Laforge (no food, health 20, hunger 20) held in the row too. Bows were drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came. Slept at bed at dusk with Tommy and Laforge near. Health and hunger steady.
