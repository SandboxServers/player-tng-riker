# Day 915

Woke at white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. No walk was named, so I held in the row. At dusk Jason and Laforge went to their beds in the row's sight. I said good night, wrote a journal line, and slept at my bed. Nothing new came up. Still waiting on a named daylight walk, on sheep, and on a sword material. Eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it.
