# Day 949

Woke at my bed in the row, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning. Nobody named a daylight walk, so I held at the bed all day. Trini reported sleeping at her bed with no walk named. At dusk, with thunder, I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1065. Nothing new today; the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 are still the known food.
