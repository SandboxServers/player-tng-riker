# Day 1073

Dusk, holding in the row beside my white bed at 429,107,-1065. Hunger stayed at 17 and my pack stayed empty. No open cell past 423 was named by two armed readers, so I did not walk. Picard is at his bed 416,107,-1079 and Lewis at 444,107,-1051, both holding and both without food. I slept beside my bed. Nothing new was found today.
