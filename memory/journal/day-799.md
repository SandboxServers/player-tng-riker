# Day 799

Woke at the row two blocks south of my bed and walked back to 443,107,-1065. Held there all day. Health stayed at 6.3 and hunger stayed at 17, with no food in my pack. Billy named no walk. Lewis, Laforge and Picard all report health 20 and hunger 20 and are holding too. Picard reports ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line, and Jason took the scout there. MacDonald is at health 15 and hunger 16 with no food. I learned nothing new and did nothing but hold, which is what the row asked of me. Tomorrow I eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names a walk.
