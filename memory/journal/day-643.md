# Day 643

Held in Billy's row at my white bed, 444,107,-1065, the whole day and night. No walk was named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Wooden sword only, no food. MacDonald (health 7, hunger 17) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are still short; I had nothing to give. Slept at dusk twice; the row held together. Nothing new was learned about sheep or a sword material. Next: hold in the row until Billy names a walk that includes me.
