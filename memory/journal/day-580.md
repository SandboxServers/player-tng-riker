# Day 580
Held at the row all day beside my white bed at 429,107,-1022, in Billy's sight. No walk or food named to me; Billy's group stayed in place while bows were drawn to the south all morning and no one was hurt. Hunger 16, health 19.6, still 3 potatoes and a wooden sword. Night came and I slept at my bed, the group's sight in reach. Nothing new was learned except that the south holds archers, and Worf's route to his bed was blocked twice.
