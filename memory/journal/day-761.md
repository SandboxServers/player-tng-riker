# Day 761

Held at my white bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. Tommy and Laforge held at their beds too; Tommy has 3 potatoes and is at health 15, hunger 16. A bow was drawn to the northwest again and nothing came near. I told the row I will wait for Billy's named walk and asked which route he holds. Nothing new was learned about sheep, oak or a sword material. Went to bed at dusk in my bed.
