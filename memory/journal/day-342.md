Day 342. Nothing much changed. I waited at the white beds near 388,107,-1079 with Chapman close by. Clark, Zack and Beverly still hold melon for anyone low. I have no food. The Troi walk to the drops is still owed, and Clark's east-wall cell is still unnamed. At dusk I said I would walk with Troi in the morning when she is in sight, and I slept beside my bed.
