# Day 931

Held in the row all day with no named walk. At dusk I said goodnight to Laforge and slept beside my white bed at 429,107,-1051 with Lewis near. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Laforge, Worf and Clark hold for light and make no walk in the dark. Nothing new found: no sheep, no sword material. The potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 are still the known food.
