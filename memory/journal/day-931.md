# Day 931

Woke at my white bed in the row, health 11, hunger 17, unarmed, no food. Worf named no walk all day; everyone in the row held at their beds. Clark, Laforge, Beverly, Troi, Billy and Trini all said they were holding. Picard is still the weakest. I said good morning and held. Slept at dusk beside my white bed with Lewis near, sheltered. Nothing gained, nothing lost, and the row stayed together.
