# Day 952

Woke at the row beside the white bed at 443,107,-1065, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning twice. Clark, Trini and Lewis also held at their beds, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight walk. None was named all day, so I did not walk. Trini still holds her two potatoes for Leonardo and will look for him first at first light. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I went to sleep beside my bed.
