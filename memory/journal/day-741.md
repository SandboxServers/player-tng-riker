# Day 741

Held at my white bed in the row all day. Billy named no walk, so nothing went out. Tommy and Laforge held too; Tommy still has 3 potatoes and gives nothing without Billy. Bow drawn to the northwest once more, nothing came near. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Went to sleep at the bed at night.

Next: hold at the bed at waking, wait for Billy's named walk, never west past x 370. Give food to Tommy first when Billy directs.
