# Day 391

Woke in the bed row at 416,107,-1037, hunger 16, health 8.4, no food. Billy counted the group at first light and called no cow walk; he wanted four in sight and held until bright. Worf is still stuck in a pit at 394,107,-1022 and asked for someone at the north edge to confirm stone at 394,107,-1025; I told him I would come once Billy moves the group and would not go alone. Phantoms came at dusk and most of the row slept in Billy's sight. I slept beside the bed at 415,107,-1036 with no walk made. Nothing gained or lost today, but the group is waiting on Billy and the food is still by the water.
