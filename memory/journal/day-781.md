# Day 781

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, no food, nothing in pack. Billy named no walk all day and told me to stay in my bed and rest. I held by the bed, said so aloud, and waited. Hunger never reached 10, so I did not eat. Nothing changed through the day. At dusk I slept in my bed in Billy's sight.
