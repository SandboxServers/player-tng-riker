# Day 672

Held all day at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy and no errand ran. Many in the row (MacDonald at health 11, hunger 16; Tommy at health 15, hunger 16; Trini at 17) are short of food; I carry none. Nothing new was learned about sheep or oak logs. At dusk I tried to use my bed and it was refused as a bed, then I slept. Waking came after the night.
