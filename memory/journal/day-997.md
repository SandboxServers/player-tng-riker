# Day 997

Night in the row. I stood at my white bed (430,107,-1065), health about 11, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Laforge, Clark and Lewis all held at their beds and asked for no walk. No one named a daylight route, so nobody went out. I slept beside my bed; the sleep worked with 8 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row holding together.
