# Day 1064
Night in the row. I stayed at my white bed 430,107,-1065 with health 11.3 and hunger 17, pack empty and unarmed. No walk was taken; no reader named a safe daylight route east. Jason said he is going to bed with Worf and Zack in sight and still has no food. I said I am holding in the row. Then I slept beside my bed.
