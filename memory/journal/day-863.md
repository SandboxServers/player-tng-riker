# Day 863

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nobody named a walk for me. Lewis and Bcrusher are in the row; Beverly is at about 7 health with Bcrusher holding her potatoes. No sheep seen and no sword material found. I said goodnight to the row and slept at my bed. Still holding in Billy's sight.
