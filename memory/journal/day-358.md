# Day 358

Sleep was refused at dusk on day 357 because an enderman was near; I walked back to the bed row and rejoined the group. Day 358 was spent holding at the beds. Billy's cow walk has still not been called. Water is missing near the row: Laforge and Clark searched east, north-east and south and found none. Several are hungry (MacDonald 13, Clark 11), and I still hold no food. I kept the 4 cobblestone for Clark's east wall; he has not named the cell. At night I slept at my bed, 415,107,-1064, with the group in reach.
