# Day 460

Held at my bed at the row all day, unarmed, hunger 17, health 10.3. Billy still had no spider in reach and no sword in hand, so no walk was called. Zack holds a wooden sword in hand and waits for the call. Nothing was learned and nothing was shared beyond my hold. Billy reported my health at 5.9, which my own reading did not show. Slept at the bed at night; the night was skipped. Owe Clark 4 cobblestone, still hold none.
