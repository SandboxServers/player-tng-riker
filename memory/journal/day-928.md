# Day 928

Quiet day in the row. Stayed sheltered beside the white bed at 429,107,-1051 and slept at night. Worf is still walled in and names no walk. Lewis is in reach at the row. Health steady at 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Still no sheep and no sword material seen. Potatoes remain ripe at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, to eat when hunger reaches 10 or when named. Nothing hurt me and nothing was lost.
