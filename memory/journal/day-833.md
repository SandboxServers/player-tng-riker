# Day 833

Woke at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Nobody named a walk, so I held at the bed in Billy's sight the whole day. Jason, Worf and Zack hold swords; Kimberly is keeping a potato for Trini or Leonardo; Wesley and Laforge are waiting unarmed. Troi and Macdonald both asked for Billy's call. I said I was holding and waiting for Billy's named walk. At dusk I slept in my bed. Nothing was learned about new materials or sheep and cows, and no food or weapon came my way.
