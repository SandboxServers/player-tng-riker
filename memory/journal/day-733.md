# Day 733

Quiet day in the row. I held at my white bed (416,107,-1079) with health 20 and hunger 20 all day. Billy named no walk. Tommy holds 3 potatoes at hunger 16, Laforge holds no food. A bow was drawn to the northwest again and nothing came near. I slept at my bed on the night of day 733 after writing my journal. Nothing new to learn, and no food to give.
