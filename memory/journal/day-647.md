Day 647. Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. No walk was named all day, so I held in the row and greeted the others each morning. A dusk sleep attempt was refused because it was not yet night; I slept at true night. The row talked a lot about the west route, with Trini, Picard and Tommy offering to escort in daylight once Billy names it. I was not named. MacDonald is at health 7 and Tommy at 15, both short of food. I have none to give.
