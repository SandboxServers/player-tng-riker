# Day 525

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 through the night while phantoms were near; no harm. Morning came, Billy never called a count, so I asked once and waited. Sword still in my pack, not in my hand. Hunger 17, health 10.3, nothing eaten. Others at the row: Clark (died in the night, has nothing), Worf, Jason, Kimberly, MacDonald, Laforge, BCrusher, Tommy. Slept at dusk at the bed; the sleep worked.
