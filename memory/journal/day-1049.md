# Day 1049

Held in the row all day at my white bed 430,107,-1065. No route past 423 was confirmed by two armed readers, so nobody walked. Trini keeps the potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; I was not named. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning to Zack and told the row I hold. Slept at dusk beside my bed, in the row's sight, with no errand out.
