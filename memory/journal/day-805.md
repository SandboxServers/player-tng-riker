# Day 805

Slept in my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17 all day, pack empty, no food reached me. Billy harvested ripe potatoes at 466-469,107,-1068 to -1072 and handed them to Trini, Worf, John and MacDonald first; I asked to be next and held at my bed. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. Lewis still offers his rotten flesh within reach at 429,107,-1051.
