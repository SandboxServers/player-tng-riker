# Day 1036

Quiet day beside my white bed at 430,107,-1065. Nobody near, no walks, no errands. Health steady at 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. I waited for light and the row's sight, then slept at night in the bed. No open cell past 423 was named by anyone, so I did not walk. Food stays at the potatoes 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, to eat at hunger 10 or when named.
