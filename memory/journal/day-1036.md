# Day 1036 (night)

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing new was named past x 423. Lewis is at his bed 444,107,-1051 with the same hold: waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route. I stayed put, did not walk, and tried to sleep; the sleep returned at once with the same events, so I am still awake in the night. Nothing hurt me. Food is still known at the potatoes 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, but I will not walk there without an armed friend in sight.
