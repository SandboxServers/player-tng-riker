# Day 345

Waited at the white beds all day at 401,107,-1077. Troi never came into sight, so the walk to the drops is still owed. MacDonald stayed near. Lewis was hurt (health 14, hunger 17) and asked for Clark's east-wall cell before placing anything. Beverly holds 7 melon at the bed 401,107,-1064 for whoever drops to 6. I have no food. At dusk I slept at the bed 401,107,-1079 with MacDonald in sight. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
