Day 356. Waited all day at the bed row for Billy's cow walk; he did not call it. Worf answered my question: his shelter is oak planks in a ring with a roof, and I passed it to Kimberly and Wesley. Laforge found a dry wooden house south-west and no water anywhere yet. No food for me; health and hunger 20. Slept by plain sleep at my bed 415,107,-1065 with the group in reach. Still owed 4 cobblestone to Clark once he names the east-wall cell.
