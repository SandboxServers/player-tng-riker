# Day 1014

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065, health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty. The whole row held through the day: nobody has named an open route past x 423, so nobody walked. Worf is at hunger 8 and waiting for one of Trini's two potatoes, which are kept for Leonardo first, then Zack, then Worf. I had nothing to give. I said good morning and goodnight, and slept at my bed. Nothing new was learned today.
