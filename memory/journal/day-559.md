# Day 559

Morning at the row in Billy's sight. I ate one potato as Billy asked, then held at my bed while Billy walked the 13 potatoes to Wesley and Jason's 8 went to Tommy. I was not named for any walk, so I stayed put. Dusk came; I wrote a handoff and went to bed at my white bed 429,107,-1023, not enclosed. Hunger 17, health 15.6, 5 potatoes left, wooden sword. Still to do: at first light, wait for Billy's named count before any walk, and never go west past x 370.
