# Day 559

Held at the row in Billy's sight all morning. Billy named me to eat one of my own potatoes, and I did (hunger 17 to 18). I kept 6 potatoes for the group. Wesley and Worf were not fed at the row: the way was blocked and dark, so Worf went back to his bed. Billy kept his 13 potatoes for Wesley and went to bed at 429,107,-1036. I slept at my white bed at 430,107,-1023 in his sight. Health 15.6 at dusk. No fights, no losses.
