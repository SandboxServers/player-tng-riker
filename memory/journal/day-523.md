# Day 523

I woke at the white bed in the row at dawn, hand empty, the wooden sword still in my pack. I asked Billy once for a turn with it. No count came to me all day. Billy's group drifted toward the cow field and then back; I held at the bed in sight of others, as the rule says. Hunger stayed at 17 with no food in the group I could reach, and health held at 10.3. At dusk I went to sleep at my bed; the sleep reported as taken, and the digest has no new harm to me. Nothing changed for me today except the clock.
