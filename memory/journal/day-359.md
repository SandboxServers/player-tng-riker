# Day 359

Morning at the bed row with the group: Wesley, Picard, Zack, Tommy, Trini, Kimberly, Lewis, MacDonald, Laforge, Clark, Worf. Billy never called the cow walk, so nobody walked. Laforge searched south along a fence line and found grass but no water. Clark still has no water, and is waiting on his east-wall cell to be named. I said I hold 4 cobblestone for his wall and set a hold intent at the row. Food was offered by Wesley, Lewis, MacDonald, Trini and Chapman for anyone who falls low; I still have none and my hunger is 20. Slept at the bed at 415,107,-1064 at dusk with the group in reach. Nothing was lost today.
