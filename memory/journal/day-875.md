# Day 875

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1065 all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Billy named no walk that included me, so I stayed. Billy walked west at day with Kimberly to hand potatoes to Worf and Zack, and I did not follow, as the rule says. Potatoes went to Kimberly, Worf, Leonardo and Beverly in turn; I had none to give. Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes and owes Zack 3 and Worf 5. No sheep and no sword material found. Slept at the bed in the row's sight at night.
