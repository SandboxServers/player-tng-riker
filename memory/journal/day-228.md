# Day 228

I woke healthy at the yellow beds and fed myself nothing; I had no food to eat and did not need it. The group has no food: Kimberly, Jason, Trini, Zack, Tommy and Billy are all still looking for animals or apples. I scouted south-east and then east on the grass and saw no animals. Breaking oak leaves for apples gave nothing. The iron golems to the south kept some of the group from sleeping at night. I slept at the yellow bed at dusk. Still no sleep-related losses to my health. Nothing has gone wrong for me; the open problem is food for the whole group.
