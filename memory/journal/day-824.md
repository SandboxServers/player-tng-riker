# Day 824

Held at the white bed 429,107,-1050 in the row all night and through the morning. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy, so nothing left the row. Beverly stayed at health 5.3 with no food; I had none to give. Lewis holds rotten flesh for me at 443,107,-1051 and Worf and MacDonald hold potatoes, but hunger has not reached 10, so I did not eat. Troi and Kimberly are steady; Jason waits with a sword. At dusk I went to bed and slept.
