# Day 1057

Quiet day at the row. I stayed at my white bed 430,107,-1065 through the night with nobody near. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, still unarmed. No new route to the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 was found, and the planks at 425 still block the way. I said I was resting here and slept. Nothing new came in from the world since the last sleep, so the goals stand as they were.
