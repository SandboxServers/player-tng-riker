# Day 1057

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady at 11.3. No daylight route to the potatoes was ever named by two armed readers, so I did not walk. Billy and Jason reported only planks and a door east of the row; no open cell has been named past them. Everyone in the row held too. Slept at my bed at night, with the row in sight.
