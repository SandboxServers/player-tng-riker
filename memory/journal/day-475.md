# Day 475

Held at the row by the white beds all day, unarmed, in sight of the group. No walk was called by Billy; the count stayed at two armed. Dusk brought phantoms onto Laforge, who was at 2 health. I tried to use the bed by hand and it failed (a bed is used by sleeping, not by a click), then I slept at night by the row, not enclosed and holding nothing. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at about 10.3.
