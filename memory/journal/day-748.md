# Day 748

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, nothing else. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody went out. Tommy (3 potatoes, hunger 16) and Laforge (no food) held at their beds too, and I told them I was with them. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice; nothing came near. Nothing new learned. Slept in the bed at dusk.
