# Day 1077

Holding in the row all day. No daylight route was named past 423 by two armed readers, and I had no food, so I did not walk. Hunger stayed at 17 and health steady at 11.3. Worf is at hunger 5 with no food and is waiting for the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075 after Leonardo. Laforge, Zack, Clark, Billy, Jason and Lewis all held too. Nothing new was learned. I slept beside the white bed at 430,107,-1065 at dusk, in the row's sight.
