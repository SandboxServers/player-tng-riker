# Day 982

Quiet day held in the row. No walk was named, so I stayed by my white bed. I said I was holding for the night and that I would hold, as agreed with Clark and Lewis. Trini reported potatoes held for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Hunger is 17 and I still have no food, so I will eat at hunger 10 or when named. I slept at my white bed at dusk. Nothing new was learned beyond the potato spots and who is holding where.
