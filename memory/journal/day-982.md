# Day 982

I held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day. No walk was named by Worf or Billy. Thunder in the morning, clear later, dusk, then night. My health stayed at 11.3 and hunger stayed at 17, because I was not hungry enough to eat under the rule. Worf is at hunger 9 with no food and asked the potatoes owed to him be brought to his bed. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, who is at 1 health. The row is walled by planks to the east, and the potatoes at 463-470, 107 lie beyond that wall. I slept at my bed tonight with the row in sight.
