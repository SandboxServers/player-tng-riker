# Day 1102

Held in the row all day at bed 430,107,-1065. Hunger stayed at 17 with an empty pack; no food reached me. The plank at 420,108,-1051 is still unnamed by a second reader, so I broke nothing. Worf (hunger 5) is walled in and Trini holds two potatoes for him. At dusk I said aloud that I was holding for the night, then slept beside the white bed. Sleep reported only 1 of 10 asleep, so the night may be restless. No damage.
