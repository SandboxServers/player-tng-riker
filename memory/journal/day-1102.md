# Day 1102

Held in the row all day at bed 430,107,-1065. Hunger stayed at 17 with an empty pack and no food reached me. Laforge, Lewis, Worf, Trini, Zack and Clark all held and broke nothing; the plank at 420,108,-1051 is still unnamed by a second reader. Worf (hunger 5) is walled in and Trini holds the two potatoes for him. I said aloud that I would hold and keep watch. At dusk I stood beside the bed, wrote the handoff, and slept without damage.
