# Day 700

Held at the white bed at 416,107,-1079 in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy and no one came near. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20, so I ate nothing and had no food to give. A bow was drawn to the northwest in the day, so I kept the wooden sword in my pack and stayed by the bed. At night I said I was going to sleep with the row, and I slept at my bed. Nothing new came of the day; the row still holds still and the open questions are where sheep graze and where a sword material comes from.
