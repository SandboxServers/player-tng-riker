# Day 878

Waking at the white bed 444,107,-1065 with health and hunger at 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Nobody came into view and no walk was named for me, so I held at the row. Billy's group split: Bcrusher and Kimberly hold at 431,107,-1047 with the potatoes, Beverly is still owed food (health 7.3, hunger 16), and the east way past 419 is blocked. Troi, Lewis and Leonardo hold at their beds and wait for Billy's word. I said good morning and asked for a walk, and nothing was asked of me. At dusk I went to bed at the row. Still no sheep, no sword material.
