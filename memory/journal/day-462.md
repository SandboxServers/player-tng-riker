# Day 462

Woke at the row bed, 444,107,-1051, holding, hunger 17, no food, health 10.3. No walk was called all day. Billy, Zack, Kimberly, MacDonald and Laforge kept talking about the sword-in-hand problem; Zack got his sword into hand with use and the wooden sword. I tried sleep in daylight and it failed. I asked Laforge by say for some potato; he has potatoes and wheat to share, but no reply yet. At dusk Billy and the others went to their beds; I went to bed by the white bed and slept. Nothing was gained or lost in danger today; the walk still waits on Billy's count.
