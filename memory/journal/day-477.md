# Day 477

Held at the row by my white bed all day, unarmed, hunger 17, health 10.3. Billy kept the count at no walk; he has no sword method yet and is trying crafting a sword as a new way. Nobody walked, no one was hurt, and no food came to me. I said my hold aloud in the morning and kept in sight of the beds. I slept in my bed at night with the group near, rested without incident. Still owe Clark 4 cobblestone; I hold none.
