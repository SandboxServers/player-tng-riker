# Day 409

I woke alone at 416,107,-1023 beside a white bed, and held still as my goal said. Billy's cow walk broke 15 blocks out, and the group went back to the bed row. Trini and Picard came near me at first light, so I walked to the row at 417,107,-1035 and counted myself in Billy's sight. Tommy offered a melon slice, but it went to Picard by mistake, and Picard holds it for whoever reaches hunger 6 first. Billy held the walk again at dusk and said to walk at first light with four in sight. I slept in the bed beside the group at 416,107,-1036. Health 8.4, hunger 16 all day, and no food eaten yet.
