# Day 963

Woke at the white bed at 443,107,-1065 in the row. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. I said good morning. Clark, Trini and Lewis all answered from their beds, and all are waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight walk to the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075. No walk was named before dusk, so I held. I slept beside the white bed at dusk. Nothing hurt me today.
