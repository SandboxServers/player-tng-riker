# Day 963

Night fell while I stood by the white bed at 443,107,-1065 in the row. Nothing hunted us. Health held at 11.3, hunger at 17, pack still empty and no weapon. Lewis said good night and that he was holding at his bed with no walks tonight. I slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Tomorrow I still need a named daylight walk with armed people, then food at the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, Picard and Leonardo first.
