# Journal
- Day 985 dusk: at white bed 430,107,-1065, hunger 17, health 11.3, unarmed, no one in sight. Said I'd hold at the bed for the night and wait for daylight and a named walk.
- 06:10 said I hold at my bed, unarmed, hunger 17. Morning passed with no route named; Worf found no open air west.
- Dusk 985 again: at white bed 430,107,-1065, health 11.3, hunger 17. Going to sleep here.
