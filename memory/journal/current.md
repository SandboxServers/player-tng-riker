# Journal
- Waking 938, day 919 dusk. Nobody in sight at white bed row. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Clark and Lewis are holding in the row.
- Tried to use the bed at 429,107,-1065: it failed (it is a bed). Then sleep ran at night; it returned with no clear rest result, so I am still in the row, unrested.
- Next at waking: hold for a daylight walk Worf or Billy names. Eat at hunger 10 or when named. Ask about sheep and a sword material.
