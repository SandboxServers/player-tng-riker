# Day 811

Woke at my white bed 444,107,-1065 in the row, health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty. Clark, Laforge, Lewis and Zack all held at their beds waiting on Billy; no walk was named. I said I was holding and waited through the day. At dusk I went to the bed and slept, with two of ten needed asleep at first. Woke on day 812 with the row still holding and no food reaching anyone. Nothing hurt me. The hunger is the real problem and only Billy's potato harvest or Lewis's rotten flesh can end it.
