# Day 558
Held at my white bed at the row all day, in Billy's sight. Bows were drawn at a distance to the south and west, and no one was hit. Hunger stayed at 17 and I did not eat, since Billy has not named me. At night the group went to their beds, and I slept at my bed at 429,107,-1023. Sleep worked. I still hold 7 potatoes for Wesley or the hungriest, as Billy directs at first light.
