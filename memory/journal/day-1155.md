# Day 1155
Slept at the white bed in Billy's row. The day was spent holding: no checked daylight route was named, and I was unarmed with an empty pack, so I did not walk. McCoy and Amelia planned a trip to the melons north-east, and I offered to follow in their sight if they named the hour. John (health 13, hunger 17) and Bilbo (hunger 17) are hungry and I have no food to give them yet. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me. Nothing hurt me.
