# Day 327

Gave my 9 planks around the beds in a long chase after Data, who could not see me for hours; they ended with Lewis. Gave my 2 oak logs to Data at last. Went hungry until Clark's melon came at dawn. I still hold 3 melon for the weakest and 4 cobblestone for Clark's wall. Worf is my neighbour at the beds. Lesson: wait for people to come into sight rather than chasing them. Bed use needs the sleep verb, not use.
