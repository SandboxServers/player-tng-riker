# Day 613

Held at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's sight all day. Billy kept the row on hold: no walk until he names a verified route. Hunger stayed at 16, health 19.6, so I did not eat, since my line is 6 or when Billy names it. Zack stood beside me with a potato and waited on Billy's call. Bows were drawn to the south all day and no harm came. At dusk I slept at my bed; sleep returned the digest with no harm.
