# Day 613

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named; the route was never verified. I stayed at my bed 429,107,-1022 with Zack beside me. Hunger stayed at 16 and I did not eat, since the rule is to eat at 6 or lower or when Billy names it. At night bows were drawn to the south and west, and I slept in my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing was gained or lost today.
