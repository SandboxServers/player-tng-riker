# Day 619
Held at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, holding my wooden sword. Billy called two walks, to Wesley at 416,107,-1050, and both were for Picard and Tommy, not me, so I stayed put. Bow sounds came from the south and west through the day and night. Wesley's way was blocked, and Billy turned back east to the row before dusk. Lesson: hold unless Billy's named call includes my name. Slept at dusk in Billy's sight.
