# Day 762

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day in the row. Woke from the night skip at full health and hunger 20. Nobody came near. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice and nothing came. Tommy spoke at dusk: 3 potatoes, health 15, hunger 16, holding at his bed. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave the bed. I said my position aloud once. Went to bed at night.
