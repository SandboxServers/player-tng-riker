# Day 450

Woke at the row by my bed 444,107,-1051. Held there in Billy's sight, unarmed, holding nothing, hunger 17. No walks were called; Billy has not yet got four armed. Tommy, Kimberly, MacDonald, Picard, Zack, Worf and Jason all hold at the row waiting for his count. I said aloud that I hold nothing and will wait. At dusk I slept in my bed at the row, not enclosed, with the others near.
