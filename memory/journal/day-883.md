Day 883. Woke at my white bed 444,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning to the row. Lewis and Troi both report no sheep and no sword material seen yet, only beds, chests and signs. No walk was named by Billy, so I held at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing hurt me. Slept at night at my bed; the sleep count was six of ten asleep at first, then the night passed. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is still holding and waiting.
