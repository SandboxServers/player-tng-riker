# Day 676

Woke at 429,107,-1063 beside a white bed, not at my old bed at 443,107,-1050. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Tried to put the wooden sword in hand by using the bed; the world refused ("that block is a bed"). No named walk came from Billy all day. The row held in place, with Tommy close by and many people speaking from their beds: Troi, Worf, MacDonald, Tommy, Jason, Zack, Trini, John, Lewis, Picard. MacDonald and Tommy are still short of food. I carry none. I slept at dusk beside my bed. The sword is still in the pack, not in hand, which is still open.
