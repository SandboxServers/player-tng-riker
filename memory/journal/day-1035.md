# Day 1035

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day. Unarmed, no food, hunger 17, health steady at 11.3. Nobody in the row named an open cell past 423, so nobody walked. Billy, Laforge, Worf and Zack all looked and saw none. I told the row I would follow a named route and not move before then. Slept at dusk beside my bed; it took two tries (2 of 10, then the bed worked). Nothing new learned today except that the row is still holding together.
