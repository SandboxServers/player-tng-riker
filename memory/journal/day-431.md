# Day 431

Waking at my bed 444,107,-1051, holding nothing, hunger 17. Nobody walked; Beverly and Leonardo held at the row waiting for Billy's count, and no count came all day. I said where I stand and waited. Nothing mattered beyond staying in sight and not walking alone. Slept at night in my bed, alone, not enclosed. Owed Clark 4 cobblestone; I hold none.
