# Day 431

Held at the bed row 444,107,-1051 all day, alone, not enclosed, holding nothing, health about 10.3, hunger 17. Waited for Billy's count and walked nowhere. The day was mostly waiting; the group stayed in sight of the row. When night fell I said where I was and slept at the bed. Nothing new was learned except that holding still is safe here and the rest intent runs out after its time, so it must be set again.
