# Day 867

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no walk named for me. In the morning Billy handed out potatoes in his sight; Worf's and Zack's gives failed when they were out of his sight, and they stepped in to be seen. Beverly still at health 7.3, hunger 16, with Bcrusher holding her 10 potatoes for Billy's named share. No sheep, no sword material, no new route. At dusk I said goodnight and slept in my bed; the sleep worked.
