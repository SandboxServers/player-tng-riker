# Day 343

Quiet day at the white beds near 388,107,-1079 with Chapman. I told him I was waiting for Troi so we could walk to the drops together; she did not come into sight, so I did not chase. Morning talk was about melon and chicken held by Picard, Beverly and Clark for anyone who drops to 6 hunger, and about Laforge's oak planks going astray to Worf. Clark's east-wall cell is still unnamed, so I placed no cobblestone. Health stayed 20 and hunger 20 all day. At night both white beds refused "use" while I stood beside them, so I slept on the open ground next to them and the sleep worked (9 of 10 asleep). I am not sheltered in a sealed space, which I should fix.
