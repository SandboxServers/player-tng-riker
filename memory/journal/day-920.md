Day 920. I woke at white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row, health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, empty pack. No walk was named all day. Worf and Zack said they had no food; Picard is at health 2.3 with nothing to eat. I had nothing to give, so I said so and held. I slept at dusk in my bed and the night passed quietly. Nothing new was found: still no sheep and no sword material.
