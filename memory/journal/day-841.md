# Day 841

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day with the row. Health 20, hunger 20, empty hands the whole day. Billy named no walk; he said he would name the next one at midday, and it did not come. Beverly (7.3 health, hunger 16) and Bcrusher (holding 20-25 potatoes, waiting to eat at hunger 6) are the weakest. Nothing spare to give. Slept at my bed at dusk with the row close by.
