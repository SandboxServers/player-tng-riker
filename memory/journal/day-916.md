# Day 916

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 the whole day, sheltered and unarmed. Worf named walks south-east and north-east; neither included me, and I said so aloud and held. The north plank at 417,107,-1052 was opened, but the north-east way was blocked and dusk came. Health stayed at 11.3 and hunger at 17, so I did not eat; no food was named for me. Slept at night in my bed, quiet. Nothing was taken from me and no one was hurt that I saw.
