# Day 862

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Said good morning to the row. Billy handed out potatoes to Zack and Worf, but no walk was named for me, so I held. Nobody needed food from me. Still no sheep and no sword material seen. Holding at the bed in Billy's sight until dusk, then went to sleep beside my bed.
