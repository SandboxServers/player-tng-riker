# Day 710

Night at the white bed 415,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Billy named no walk. Tommy and Laforge both hold their beds and wait on Billy. Tommy holds 3 potatoes owed to MacDonald. I held my place, said where I stood, and wrote one note. The sleep did not take at night; the day stays 710. Next: hold at the bed, eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it, never west past x 370.
