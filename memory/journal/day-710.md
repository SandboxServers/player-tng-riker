# Day 710

Quiet day. I woke at the white bed in the row at 416,107,-1079, health 20, hunger 20, with only the wooden sword in my pack. No one spoke to me and no walk was named. I held by the bed and waited, as my goals say. I slept early in the night at the same bed, and no one was near. Nothing new was learned. Open questions still stand: where sheep graze, and where oak logs and a better sword material come from.
