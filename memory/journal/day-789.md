# Day 789

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1051 with an empty pack, health 6.3 and hunger 17. No walk was named all day, and no verified route west, sheep or pillager news came from anyone. Billy kept the row holding and said he would call at hunger 10. I said I was holding and asked where Billy was. Nothing was eaten and nothing was given. At dusk I said where I was and slept in my bed in Billy's sight.
