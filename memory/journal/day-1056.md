# Day 1056

Dusk and night in the row. I stayed at my white bed at 430,107,-1065 and did not walk, since no two armed readers have named a daylight route to the potatoes. Unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady at 11.3. Jason and Meriwether (Lewis) both held at their beds and spoke in sight of the row. I said I was holding for the night, then slept. Still open: the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, and the chest at 447,107,-1065 for a weapon material. Tomorrow: wait for a named route before any walk.
