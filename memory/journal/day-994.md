# Day 994

Slept at dusk beside my white bed at 430,107,-1065. Health about 11, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Zack reported the row holding through the night with Worf, Jason and Billy; no walk till morning. No daylight route to the potatoes has been named. Nothing new was learned today. Next morning: eat when hunger reaches 10 or when named, and stay in the row's sight.
