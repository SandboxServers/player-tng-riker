# Day 790

Day 790 was quiet. I held at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row, in sight of Billy. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no food and no weapon. Billy named no walk: no verified route west, no sheep seen, pillagers still west and south. Johnny also had no sheep and no sword. I told the row I was holding and had no food. At night I slept in my bed. Nothing new was found; the row is waiting on Billy's word and on food.
