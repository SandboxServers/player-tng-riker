# Day 790

Woke at a spot 13 blocks off from my bed, walked back to my white bed at 443,107,-1051 and held there. No walk was named all day. Billy said food is gone from the row and no route west is verified, so everyone holds at the beds. My hunger stayed at 17, above the feed line of 10, so I did not eat. Health steady at 6.3. Nothing new was learned about sheep, swords or food. I slept at my bed at night with Billy, Lewis and MacDonald in sight.
