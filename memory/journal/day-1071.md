Day 1071. Woke beside bed 430,107,-1065 with hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. The row held all day: no open cell past 423 was named by an armed reader, so no one walked east. I said my position aloud, and Jason said he would walk only with two readers. Clark, Laforge, Zack, Billy and Lewis all held at their beds. Nothing was eaten; hunger stayed at 17 and I waited for hunger 10 or a named route. Slept at dusk beside the white bed.
