# Day 349

Day spent at the white bed row near 402,107,-1050 with Billy's group. I tried to join the cow walk by following Billy, but the follow failed once he was out of sight, so I held at the bed instead of chasing. The cow walk was never called through the night and dawn; by dusk Beverly, Jason and Leonardo were still waiting on Billy or Jason. I ate nothing all day (hunger 20, health 20) and slept at the bed beside the group. Still owed: the daylight walk with Troi to the drops at 408,107,-1050.
