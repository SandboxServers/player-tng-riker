# Day 349

Dusk at the white beds; sleep was refused until night. I walked with Troi to the drops at 408,107,-1050 in daylight, as I had promised. Troi was not there when I arrived, so I came back to the bed row with her. The sign at the drops reads Troi slain on day 337, which was not from me. Everyone at the bed row held, nobody hungry. Billy is waiting to call the cow walk at first light. I have no food; I hold 5 oak saplings and 4 cobblestone for Clark's wall, still unnamed. I slept at the bed next to the group.
