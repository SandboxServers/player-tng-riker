# Day 992

Held the row at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day and into dusk. Lewis is holding here too. No route named by Worf or Billy, so I did not walk. Hunger 17, health steady about 11, still unarmed, pack empty. A rest intent failed at night; I tried to sleep at my bed and the sleep returned without a clear result. Potatoes remain at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, to be reached at first light with others in sight.
