# Day 992

Woke in the row at night, dim and clear, health about 11, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing threatened. Laforge was resting at the row bed and said he was holding still with no walk. I stayed beside my white bed at 430,107,-1065, said I was holding the row, and slept. Nobody named a daylight route, so I did not walk. Food is still at the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, and I eat at hunger 10 or when named.

Next: when day comes, check the row with Worf or Billy before any walk, and eat before hunger reaches 10.
