# Day 992

Held at my white bed in the row all day. No daylight route was named by Worf or Billy; the north-west gap is still walled past the row. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at about 11, no food and no weapon. Everyone in the row held in sight and nobody walked. At dusk I slept beside my bed at 430,107,-1065 and woke with nothing changed. Food still waits at the potatoes 463-470, 107, -1068 to -1075, and Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack.
