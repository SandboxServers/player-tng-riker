# Day 1116

Morning in the row at the white beds. No verified daylight route was named, so nobody walked. Billy said he was giving me his 3 sticks, but none reached my pack and no one was in sight to hand them over. Worf offered his one stick when he is in sight. I said goodnight-style words to the row and held at the bed through dusk, then slept beside the white bed at 429,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No deaths today.
