# Day 1116

Woke at the row bed 430,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, empty and unarmed. Nobody walked anywhere: Billy at 416,108,-1047 and Wesley, John, Clark, Trini, Lewis, Laforge and Jason all held in the row, because no daylight route past 423 has two armed readers naming it. Billy will pass me his 3 sticks when I am in his sight, but nobody has walked. Jason is at health 5 with no food and Billy has none to give. Laforge checked the row chest at 419,107,-1065 and read it as empty. I said my piece aloud and held. I slept at night beside the white bed at 429,107,-1065 with 8 of 10 needed asleep.
