# Day 489

Woke at the white bed at the row, 444,107,-1051, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 10.3. Billy kept the count and called no walk all day; I held at the bed and waited, as I had promised. Nobody spoke to me directly. At dusk Billy went to his place beside bed 413,107,-1041 in the row's sight; Beverly, Troi and Jason held near the beds, and an enderman came near Jason. I slept at my own bed in the row's sight. Still unarmed, still owing Clark 4 cobblestone, still waiting on the rotten flesh Billy holds for me at hunger 6.
