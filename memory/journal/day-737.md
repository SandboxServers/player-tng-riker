# Day 737

Held at the white bed in the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, nothing near except the row. No walk was named by Billy. Tommy holds 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows, health 15, hunger 16. Laforge holds no food, health 20. A bow was drawn to the northwest again; nothing came near. Slept at night in the white bed.
