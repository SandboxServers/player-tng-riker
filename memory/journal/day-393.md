Day 393. Woke at the bed row beside 415,107,-1037, health 8.4, hunger 16. Said good morning and counted with the group. Billy held the row all day and never called the cow walk, though Lewis stepped into his view and he counted four. No food was eaten; Jason and Trini offered flesh for when the walk starts. Worf is still boxed in the pit at 394,107,-1022. At dusk the group went to bed in the row and I lay down beside the bed. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt.
