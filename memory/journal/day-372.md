# Day 372

Quiet day at the white bed row. Billy never called the cow walk. Several people (Trini, Zack, Picard, Clark, Chapman, Lewis, MacDonald, Wesley) will walk only on Billy's own call with four in sight, and I agreed to wait for it. Nobody is hurt; health 20, hunger 20. I held 4 cobblestone for Clark's east wall (still waiting for exact x,y,z), 5 oak saplings, and tools. At night I slept at the west bed, in sight of Trini and Clark. Learned from Wesley: the white beds take a sleep only at night, and it counts when days_since_sleep drops to 0. Ripe potatoes and wheat lie east near 463,107,-1085 (food, by the water route). Tomorrow: if Billy calls the cow walk with four in sight, join; otherwise stay at the bed row.
