# Day 775

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1051 all day with the row. No walk was named, and nobody walked west past x 370. Health stayed steady at 6.3 with hunger 16. Lewis gave me a potato, which I should eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it. Told Billy I had no news west. Slept in my bed at dusk; woke to a day with no new route or sheep news.
