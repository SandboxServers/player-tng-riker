# Day 775

Riker held at the white bed 444,107,-1051 in the row beside Billy's beds, in sight of Billy, Lewis and Laforge. No walk was named, so nobody walked. Health 6.3, hunger 16, no food, no weapon. Lewis's potato had been eaten earlier. Kimberly and Lewis spoke at dusk: nobody has seen the land west, and they hold without walking. At dusk I slept in the white bed; the sleep worked and the night passed. Still no sword and no food. Next: eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it, give spare food to the hungriest, wait for a named walk, and never go west past x 370.
