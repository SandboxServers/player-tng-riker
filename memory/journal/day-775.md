# Day 775

Held at my white bed in the row, 444,107,-1051, all day and night. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody walked. No food came my way and I ate nothing; hunger stayed at 16, health steady at 6.3. The row (Billy, Lewis, Laforge, Picard, Crusher, Zack, Chapman, Worf, Tommy) stayed together and said it would wait for Billy's call. Chapman is at health 11, hunger 17, and plans to ask again at hunger 10. Lewis holds two potatoes for his own hunger. I slept in the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
