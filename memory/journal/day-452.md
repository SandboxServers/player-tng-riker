# Day 452

Woke at the row in the dark with nothing in hand and hunger 17. Billy refused use of his sword at his spot, and no walk was called all day, so nobody moved. I held at my bed 444,107,-1051, unarmed and in sight of the row, and said so aloud. Kimberly, Tommy, MacDonald and Zack held nearby. Nothing changed for me except the light. At dusk I slept at my bed; the sleep worked with 9 of 10 asleep. I did not eat, since hunger is still above 6.
