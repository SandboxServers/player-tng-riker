# Day 680

Held in the row at my bed, 416,107,-1079, all day. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. No walk was named by Billy, who has not been seen since day 676. Speech from Tommy, MacDonald, Laforge, Picard, Jason, Trini, John and Zack all said the same: holding for Billy's named call. A bow was drawn to the northwest once; nothing came near. I said my own holding line. Nothing new learned about sheep, oak or a sword material. Went to bed at night in the row beside my bed.
