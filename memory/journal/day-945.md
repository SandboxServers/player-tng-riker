# Day 945

Woke at the white bed in the row, said good morning. Health 11.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. No walk was named all day. Trini is armed and still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf. Lewis and Clark also held at their beds. Nothing was gained or lost. I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1065 at night, in the row's sight. Next: hold for a named daylight walk with armed people in sight; eat at hunger 10 or when named.
