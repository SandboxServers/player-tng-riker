# Day 530

Held at my white bed in the row, in Billy's sight, all day. No count from Billy came. The row stayed unarmed and waiting; Worf and Laforge reported the same. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 10.3, with no food reachable. I said once aloud that I wait for Billy's count and I did not walk. Slept at the white bed at dusk.
