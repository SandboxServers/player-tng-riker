# Day 530

Nobody has called a count yet. The row holds at the white beds in Billy's sight, unarmed, with no food. I stayed at the white bed 443,107,-1051 through the night and the dawn. Hunger is 17 and health 10.3, and nothing has been eaten. I asked Billy once for my wooden sword in hand, and asked Beverly once for bread or melon. Beverly has none. I slept at dusk at the white bed as my goal says.
