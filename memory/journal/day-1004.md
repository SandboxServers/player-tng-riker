# Day 1004

Held by the row all day at my white bed, 430,107,-1065. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not go east or west. Health steady near 11, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Worf and Lewis are holding by their beds through the night. Worf will look at first light for open cells toward Trini's bed. I slept beside my bed at night as my rule says. Next: at light, listen for a named route; eat at hunger 10.
