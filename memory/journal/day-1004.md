# Day 1004

Held with the row all day at my white bed, 430,107,-1065. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not go east. Health 11, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Worf, Jason, Laforge, Trini and Clark all held too. Trini still holds two potatoes for Zack and Worf. At night I said goodnight to the row and slept beside my bed. Nothing new was learned today except that the east side past x 420 is still unseen.
