# Day 835

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day and into the night. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Nothing spare to give; I hold no food or sword. Beverly (health 7.3, hunger 16) is still the weakest and needs food first. Said aloud that I was holding for Billy's word. Slept in my bed at night.
