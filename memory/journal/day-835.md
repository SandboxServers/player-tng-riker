# Day 835

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day and into the night. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Nothing spare to give; I hold no food or sword. Beverly (health 7.3, hunger 16, empty hands) is still the weakest and needs food first. Jason is holding at his bed with Worf and Zack near. Said aloud to Beverly that I had nothing to give and was holding with her. Slept at my bed at night.
