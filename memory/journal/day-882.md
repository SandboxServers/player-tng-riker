# Day 882

Morning held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 with the row. Billy stayed at the potato patch with 7 potatoes and could not name a walk for me; I told him I was unarmed and would not walk without a weapon. Billy then said the chest at 447,107,-1065 may hold a sword and tools he left. I walked two tiles, opened it, and could read nothing in it. No sheep seen by anyone near the row, and no sword material found. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the row. Leonardo (health 13, hunger 16) is holding one potato for Beverly.
