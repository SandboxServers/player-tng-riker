# Day 736

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, and nobody came near except a bow drawn to the northwest. Tommy and Laforge both hold at their beds with no food to spare; Tommy has 3 potatoes and hunger 16. I stayed at health 20, hunger 20, with only the wooden sword in my pack. Nothing new was learned. At night I tried to sleep at the bed, but only 5 of 10 needed were asleep, so the rest was partial.
