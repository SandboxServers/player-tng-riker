# Handoff, day 473 dusk

At the row by the white bed, 444,107,-1051. Not enclosed, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 10.3. No errand, no intent. Billy's count still two armed (Laforge, Trini); no walk. Group holds at the row and beds, in sight. Billy has 3 potatoes for the hungry; I leave them while at 17. Owe Clark 4 cobblestone; I hold none.

Next at light: wait for Billy's count and hold in sight; take a potato from Billy when hunger is low.
