# Handoff, night 541

Asleep at my white bed 443,107,-1037, in Billy's sight at the row. Health 10.3, hunger 17, no food held. Not enclosed. Nothing half-done.
Wooden sword still in pack, not in hand. Tried use on my bed again at night 540: "that block is a bed." Billy says he will try to hand me a sword that goes into hand in the morning.
Billy's walk is for the armed four only; I hold at the row.
Next: at waking, wait for Billy's count; eat when food comes or hunger is 6 or lower; ask Billy about the sword hand-off.
