# Handoff, dusk 1059

At my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row. No errand out, not in danger. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Still no open cell named past the planks at 425; no potato route named by two readers. Everyone in the row is holding. Next at light: view west again, hold unless two armed readers name an open route, eat at hunger 10 or when named.
