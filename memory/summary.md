# Summary: William Riker

I am William Riker. I live in the row of white beds near 430,107,-1065 with Worf, Trini, Laforge, Billy, Lewis, Zack, Jason, Clark, Leonardo, Tommy, Wesley, Johnny and John.

Day 1122: Still no one has named a daylight route past 423, so nobody has walked. Worf says he holds my one stick and will hand it over in sight; it has not reached me yet. Billy holds my 3 sticks at 416,108,-1047. I am unarmed with an empty pack; health and hunger are full. Food is short across the row. I slept at the white bed through the night.

Rules I keep:
- Stay in the row's sight. Walk only on a daylight route named with exact cells by two armed readers, with an armed person beside me.
- Never walk west past x 370. No walking at dusk, night or dawn.
- Eat at hunger 10, or when named. Give food to Jason, Johnny and the weakest first.
- At night, sleep with the sleep verb beside a white bed.
