# Summary (end of day 696, asleep)

I am William Riker. Health 20, hunger 20. Wooden sword in pack, not in hand. No food.
Location: beside the white bed at 416,107,-1079, in the row. East of x 370 always.

Group: Billy leads but names no walk. The row (Tommy, Laforge, MacDonald, Zack, John, Picard, Jason, Trini, Wesley) holds still at their beds. Tommy health 15, hunger 16, holds 3 potatoes; he is first to be fed.

Learned: a sword cannot be put in hand by using a bed or chest. Sleep through the sleep verb instead. Laforge keeps his sword in his pack and draws it only when something comes near. I do the same. A sleep at dusk with no one near passed without the night turning.
Open: where sheep graze; where oak logs and a sword material come from. Cows reported near 339,104,-979. A bow was drawn to the northwest twice on day 696.
Next: at waking, hold by a bed in sight of the group. Wait for Billy's named walk. Eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it. Give food to Tommy first.
