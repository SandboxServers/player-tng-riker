# Summary (end of day 719, slept at bed)

I am William Riker. Health 20, hunger 20. Wooden sword in pack, not in hand. No food.
Location: white bed at 416,107,-1079, in the row. East of x 370 always.

Group: Billy names no walk. Tommy holds 3 potatoes (health 15, hunger 16), eats below 15 or on Billy's call. Laforge holds at bed 430,107,-1065, no food.
Bow drawn to the northwest at night on day 719; nothing came near.

Learned: plain wait is the way to pass time; sleep at night at my bed.
Open: where sheep graze; where oak logs and a sword material come from. Cows reported near 339,104,-979.
Next: hold at bed at waking. Wait for Billy's named walk. Eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it. Give food to Tommy first.
