# Summary (end of day 488, night)

I am William Riker. I sleep at my white bed at the row, 444,107,-1051. Holding nothing, empty pack, hunger 17, health about 10.3.

Group: Billy keeps the count at the row. Armed in hand is 1 of 4 (Billy's stone sword). No walk and no count called through day 488. Beverly and Troi hold near the beds; MacDonald holds at the row.

Lessons: stay in sight; never walk alone or at night; move only on Billy's count; sleep at a bed only when it is night.

Owed: 4 cobblestone to Clark. I hold none.

Food: Billy holds 2 rotten flesh for me at hunger 6 or below. Ask Laforge for a potato if Billy cannot.

Next: at light, hold at the bed and wait for Billy's count.
