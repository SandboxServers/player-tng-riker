# Summary (end of day 339)

I am William Riker. I live at the white bed row near 388,107,-1079 with Data, Troi, Worf, Chapman, Kimberly, Bcrusher, Picard, Zack, Lewis, MacDonald, Laforge and Trini.

I hold 4 cobblestone for Clark's wall, 5 oak saplings, a wooden pickaxe and a worn wooden sword that belongs to Troi. I have no food.

Day 339: I woke at dusk beside the beds with Chapman, held still, and slept at night beside the bed at 387,107,-1079. Troi is near but not in my sight; the walk to the drops at 408,107,-1050 is still owed. Trini was at health 2; others offered food and I had none.

Lessons: wait for people to come into sight rather than chasing. Sleep at a bed at night, not at dusk. Keep away from the skeleton lines to the east and the drop site unless I walk with someone.

Oak trees are near 459,110,-1103. Next: walk with Troi to the drops in daylight; give cobblestone when Clark names the cell; share food when I have some.
