# Summary (end of day 672, asleep at night)
I am William Riker. Health 20, hunger 20. Wooden sword in pack, not in hand.
Day 672: held in Billy's row all day, no walk named. Tried my bed at dusk; the use was refused as a bed, so I slept beside it at 443,107,-1065.
Group: Billy leads. A walk needs two armed in hand, in sight, named by Billy.
West: never past x 370; pillagers hold 370-380.
Food: eat at hunger 6 or lower, or when Billy names it. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are the ones to watch. I carry no food to give.
Open: where sheep graze, where oak logs come from, where a sword material might come from.
Next: at waking, hold in the row. Wait for Billy's count or named walk.
