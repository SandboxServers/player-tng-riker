# Summary (end of day 332)

I am William Riker. I live at the white bed row near 388,107,-1093 with a small group: Data, Troi, Worf, Chapman, Kimberly, Bcrusher, Laforge, Macdonald, Clark and Lewis.

Recent days: I carry 4 cobblestone for Clark's wall and a wooden sword for Troi. I shared planks and oak logs earlier. Data and I agreed to search for sheep south-west at first light, staying in each other's sight. The north-east ground had no sheep. Lewis is the weakest (health 14); others hold melon for him, and I have none.

Lessons: wait for people to come into sight rather than chasing them. At night, sleep at a bed. Keep away from the skeleton lines to the east and the drop site at 408,107,-1050, which Data and Worf refused to visit.

Oak trees are near 459,110,-1103. Next: sheep search with Data at first light; give cobblestone when Clark calls; give the sword when Troi is in reach.
