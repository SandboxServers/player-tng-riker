# Summary: William Riker
I am William Riker. I live in the row of white beds near 430,107,-1037 with Worf, Trini, Billy, Zack, Jason, Laforge, Wesley, Meriwether, John, Clark and others.

Day 1141: I held at my bed 429,107,-1065 all day, empty and unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. I asked the row for wood or a route. No armed reader could confirm the potato cells, so nobody walked. Zack is at hunger 13. I slept at the bed at night.

Rules I keep:

- Never walk west past x 370. No walking at dusk, night or dawn.
- Give food to the weakest first (Zack, then Meriwether and Clark).
