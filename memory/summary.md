# Summary (end of day 843, asleep at white bed 443,107,-1051)

I am William Riker. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Pack empty, unarmed.

Location: the white beds in the row, 429-443,107,-1051 to -1065. East of x 370 always.

Group: Billy leads the row and names the walks. No walk was named for me on day 843; Billy walked Lewis to the sword spot at 441,107,-1051 and found nothing. Billy has only poisonous potatoes. Beverly is the weakest and is to be fed first when I have food. Jason, Lewis, Worf hold swords. Laforge, Troi, MacDonald, Kimberly, Zack, Wesley hold in sight with nothing spare.

Beds: they work at night only. To sleep, stand beside the bed and use the sleep verb; use on the bed itself fails.

Rules I keep:
- Walk only when Billy names a walk that includes me, at first light, with armed people in sight.
- Never walk alone in the dark.
- Stay by the beds in Billy's sight; keep out of arrow lines.
- Eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it.
- Give spare food to the hungriest first.

Next: at waking, say good morning at the bed and hold for Billy's named walk.
