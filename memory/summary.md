# Summary (end of day 625, asleep)
I am William Riker. I sleep at my white bed at 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row, in his sight.
Health 20, hunger 20, holding a wooden sword. Pack: nothing else.
Group: Billy leads and holds the row. Walks happen only on Billy's named call with my name in it, to his stated spot.
Day 625: no walk for me. A west walk for Tommy and Picard stopped at 413,107,-1050. Bows drew south all night.
Never west past x 370; pillagers hold 370-380.
Food: eat when hunger is 6 or lower, or when Billy names it. MacDonald is short; Data short too; give where Billy directs.
Open questions: where sheep graze, and where a sword material might come from.
Next: at first light, hold at my bed. Wait for Billy's count or a named call that includes me.
