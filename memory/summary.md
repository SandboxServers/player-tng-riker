# Summary (end of day 489, night)

I am William Riker. I sleep at my white bed at the row, 444,107,-1051. Holding nothing, empty pack, hunger 17, health about 10.3.

Group: Billy keeps the count at the row, and holds at his place beside bed 413,107,-1041. No walk and no count called through day 489. Beverly, Troi and Jason hold near the beds in the row's sight. An enderman came near Jason at dusk.

Lessons: stay in sight; never walk alone or at night; move only on Billy's count; sleep at a bed only at night.

Owed: 4 cobblestone to Clark. I hold none.

Food: Billy holds 2 rotten flesh for me at hunger 6 or below. Ask Laforge for a potato if Billy cannot.

Next: at light, hold at the bed and wait for Billy's count.
