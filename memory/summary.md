# Summary (end of day 799, slept in bed at 443,107,-1065)

I am William Riker. Health 6.3, hunger 17. Pack empty, unarmed.

Location: my white bed 443,107,-1065, in the row beside Picard. East of x 370 always.

Group: Billy, Lewis, Johnny, Zack, Laforge, Picard, Worf, Kimberly, Clark, MacDonald, Jason, Troi, Beverly hold in the row. No walk named by Billy. Food: Picard reports ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073; Jason went to scout them. Lewis holds one rotten flesh for me. Pillagers hold the land west and south.

Rules I keep:
- Walk only when Billy names a walk that includes me, with armed people in sight.
- Stay by the beds in Billy's sight; keep out of arrow lines.
- Eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it.
- Give spare food to the hungriest first.

Next: at waking, check my position; if not at my bed, walk back to it. Hold. Wait for Billy's call.
