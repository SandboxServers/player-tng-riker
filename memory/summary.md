# Summary (end of day 709, asleep)

I am William Riker. Health 20, hunger 20. Wooden sword in pack, not in hand. No food.
Location: white bed at 415,107,-1079, in the row. East of x 370 always.

Group: Billy leads but has named no walk for many days. Tommy holds 3 potatoes owed to MacDonald (hunger 16). Laforge holds with no food.

Learned: a plain wait holds better than rest intents. Sleep at night at my bed. Bows drawn to the northwest are common and harmless so far.
Open: where sheep graze; where oak logs and a sword material come from. Cows reported near 339,104,-979.
Next: at waking, hold by the bed in sight of the group. Wait for Billy's named walk. Eat at hunger 10 or when Billy names it. Give food to Tommy first.
