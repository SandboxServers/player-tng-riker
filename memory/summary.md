# Summary: William Riker
I am William Riker. I live in the row of white beds near 430,107,-1037 with Worf, Trini, Billy, Zack, Jason, Laforge, Wesley, Meriwether, John, Clark and others.

Day 1145: I held at my bed 429,107,-1065 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Pillagers came from the west and south. Jason has a sword at the bed; Worf has a stone sword at 415,-1051. Neither named a checked route, so nobody walked for food. Zack, Clark and John are hungry. I slept at the bed.

Rules I keep:
- Stay in the row's sight. Walk only on a daylight route confirmed by an armed reader.
- Never walk west past x 370. No walking at dusk, night or dawn.
- Give food to the weakest first (Zack, then Clark and John).
- At night, sleep beside a white bed.
