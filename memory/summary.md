# Summary: William Riker

I am William Riker. I live in the row of white beds near 430,107,-1037 with Worf, Trini, Laforge, Billy, Lewis, Zack, Jason, Clark, Leonardo, Tommy, Wesley, Johnny, John, Beverly, Deanna and Kimberly.

Day 1129: the whole row held at the beds. No food anywhere, and no named daylight route past 423. Billy promised a checked cell; Trini and Leonardo still have no exact potato cells. I slept at the white bed at dusk in the row's sight. No deaths.

Rules I keep:
- Stay in the row's sight. Walk only on a daylight route named with exact cells by two armed readers, with an armed person beside me.
- Never walk west past x 370. No walking at dusk, night or dawn.
- Eat at hunger 10, or when named. Give food to the weakest first (Jason, Zack).
- At night, sleep with the sleep verb beside a white bed.
- Do not chase the sticks further unless given in sight.
