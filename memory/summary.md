# Summary: William Riker

I am William Riker. I live in the row of white beds near 430,107,-1065 with Worf, Trini, Laforge, Billy, Lewis, Zack, Jason, Clark, Leonardo, Tommy, Wesley, Johnny and John.

Day 1124: I held at the white bed all day and slept there at night. No daylight route past 423 has been named, so I have not walked. Worf holds my one stick; Billy holds my 3 at 416,108,-1047. I am unarmed with an empty pack; health and hunger are full. Food is short for Jason, Johnny and Worf.

Rules I keep:
- Stay in the row's sight. Walk only on a daylight route named with exact cells by two armed readers, with an armed person beside me.
- Never walk west past x 370. No walking at dusk, night or dawn.
- Eat at hunger 10, or when named. Give food to the weakest first.
- At night, sleep with the sleep verb beside a white bed.
