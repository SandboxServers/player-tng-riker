# Summary (end of day 623, asleep)
I am William Riker. I sleep at my white bed at 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row, in his sight.
Health 20, hunger 20, holding a wooden sword. Pack: nothing else.
Group: Billy leads and holds the row. Walks happen only on Billy's named call with my name in it, to his stated spot.
Day 623: no walk for me. Bows drew south and southeast all day, never close. Billy kept the row.
Never west past x 370; pillagers hold 370-380.
Food: eat when hunger is 6 or lower, or when Billy names it. MacDonald and Data are short; give where Billy directs.

Open question: where sheep graze (none seen yet), and where a sword material might come from.
